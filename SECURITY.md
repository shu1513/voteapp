# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **contact@electionssimplified.com** with a description, steps to reproduce, and the impact you see. Do not open a public issue for security problems.

You will get an acknowledgement within 3 days and a fix or a timeline within 14 days for confirmed issues. We will credit you in the fix unless you ask us not to.

## Scope

- This repository and the deployed site at electionssimplified.com.
- Anything that exposes user accounts, ballot drafts, or member payment state, or that lets someone alter election data shown to voters.

Out of scope: rate-limit findings without a working bypass, reports from automated scanners with no reproduction, and issues in third-party services we depend on (report those upstream).

## Supported versions

Only the `main` branch and the current deployment receive fixes.
