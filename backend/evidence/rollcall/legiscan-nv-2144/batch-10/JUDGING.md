## SB 443
Section 3: prevailing wage applies to construction by a public utility on a
"significant operational or capital requirement project", meaning a natural gas
pipeline replacement approved by the Public Utilities Commission, or a new
generation plant of at least 50 megawatts. It does not apply to an electric
utility's project when the primary contractor has a collective bargaining,
project labor or worksite agreement. Applies to contracts from October 1, 2025.

## SB 161
Adds a binding arbitration option for teachers' unions and school districts when
a contract has expired or is about to expire near the start of the school year.
While an expired contract stays in force, a district may not require longer
work. Penalties for an illegal strike fall on the union and its officers, not on
individual teachers, and action at one or a few schools is not a strike unless
it is district-wide.

## SB 298
"Punitive action" against a peace officer now includes denial of a seniority or
compensation increase. An agency may not deny such an increase during an
investigation unless the investigation may lead to dismissal or demotion, or a
collective bargaining agreement provides otherwise.
