# Iowa batch-06 — judging notes

Sources: the enrolled act on legis.iowa.gov with Iowa's drawn strike and underline lines
resolved, the Legislative Services Agency explanation for triage only, and the bill history.
No sponsor material.

## HF 316 — career education (House 61-28) — public_education_quality, yea for
Section 2 restructures the grades one through six subject list and adds career planning and
pathways in grades five and six, plus communicable diseases in health. Sections 1 and 3 create
the state list of industry-recognized credentials, a transcript seal, confidential district
reporting, and recognition of graduates who earn one. Every strand adds instruction or
recognition.

## SF 378 — speed limits (Senate 28-16) — public_safety_and_crime_control, yea against
Section 3 strikes fifty-five and inserts sixty as the default limit where no other limit
applies. Sections 1 and 2 raise the top of the zone range in which the first two minor
speeding tickets in a year do not count toward suspension, from 55 to 60 miles per hour.
Section 6 lets agencies use stickers on existing signs. Higher default speeds and fewer
tickets counting both run against traffic safety; whether that trade is worth it is the
contested part, not its direction.

Review fix: the default limit applies wherever no other speed restriction applies, posted or
statutory (section 3 reads "unless otherwise provided in this section or by other speed
restrictions"), so "no other limit is posted" overstated it. The minor-ticket zone range is
35 to 60 (the act writes it as "between thirty-four and sixty-one"), so the description now
states both ends instead of "up to 60".

## HF 2670 — curriculum and testing (House 65-28, Senate 30-17) — civil_rights, yea against
Section 6 strikes "multicultural, and gender-fair" from the accreditation standard and the
sentence requiring global perspectives at all levels. That strand carries the label, on the
same reasoning as HF 2711 and HF 856 in earlier batches. The act's education-quality strands
pull both ways, since section 2 adds social studies testing while section 7 removes emotional
and social habits and human growth and development from kindergarten. Under the one-label-
per-strand rule that strand gets no label, and the description states all of it.

Review fix: section 20 delays only the social studies assessment to school years beginning
July 1, 2027; the science grade change and the rest take effect on the normal date. The
descriptions said students must "now" take the test; they now name the 2027-28 start.
