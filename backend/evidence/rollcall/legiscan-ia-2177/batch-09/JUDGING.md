# Iowa batch-09 — judging notes

Sources: the SF 2263 and SF 2284 reprints, floor amendments S-5021, H-8290, H-8321, H-8366,
S-5192 and S-5242 from legis.iowa.gov (AMDI), and the bill histories. No sponsor material.

- **SF 2263, Senate 35-12 and House 60-26** — gun_control, yea against, both chambers. Guns in
  locked vehicles in public parking lots, lawful guns in foster-care vehicles, and the weapons
  free zone label removed (Senate) or renamed (House) with double fines kept. The House version
  adds an anti-red-flag division with a $50,000 penalty per case, the HF 2718 text widened to
  violent aggravated misdemeanors. Fate: the chambers never agreed on the same text.
- **SF 2284, Senate 31-9** — data_privacy, yea for. License plate readers need a local
  ordinance, cannot use facial recognition, keep images at most 30 days unless tied to a criminal
  case, log and audit every search, and cannot feed marketing. The mobile traffic camera caps and
  revenue split, and the owner's defense when someone else was driving, are described but carry
  no label: limiting traffic cameras has no defensible direction on public safety. Fate: the
  House passed its own version 77-11 and the chambers never agreed on the same text.
