# Iowa batch-10: labor measures reopened (labor phase C)

LegiScan session 2177. **4 roll calls, 2 measures, 192 records, 101 candidates.**

Each measure was dropped or held in an earlier batch because no research area
covered labor or union rights. The `labor_rights` area now exists (migration
277, PR #1294), so each was reread in its enrolled text and judged under the
scope rules in `labor-rights-backfill-2026-09-10/NOTES.md`. Both became law.

## Finality

- SF 472: the Senate passed it unamended in 2025, and the House passed the same
  text in 2026 after rejecting two amendments. Both rolls are on the enacted text.
- SF 603: the House amended the bill (H-1106) and passed it 64-34. The Senate
  concurred and passed the House version 32-15 (roll 1524540). The same-day
  concurrence motion, roll 1521855, also 32-15, is acknowledged in the
  judgments. The Senate's first vote, 48-0, was on the earlier text.

## Scope notes

- SF 603 also reorganizes the workforce board and changes the unemployment
  taxable wage definition. Those parts are out of scope (general unemployment
  rules stay under social programs). The label rests on Division III, which
  bars cities and counties from setting contractor pay or training requirements,
  and makes apprenticeship training voluntary. The description names both.
- SF 607 stays out of scope.
