## SF 472
Section 20.15(2)(a) keeps the rule that the labor board holds a retention and
recertification election before each contract expires. New subparagraphs (3)
to (7): after notice, the public employer must send a list of unit employees
within 10 days; the board publishes a list of employers that have not; any Iowa
resident may seek a writ of mandamus to force the list, with costs and fees to
the winner; and the board extends election timelines while that is resolved.
The bill enforces the elections a union must win to keep representing workers,
so a yes vote is `against`.

## SF 603
Division III adds section 84D.8 (apprenticeship training is voluntary unless
state law requires it, and local governments may add no apprenticeship
conditions) and new limits in sections 274.3, 331.301 and 364.3: school
districts, counties and cities may not impose requirements on developers,
contractors or subcontractors about their employees' compensation or training
beyond what state law allows, or tie incentives to them. That removes local
wage standards for contractors, so a yes vote is `against`. Divisions I and II
(workforce board duties and the unemployment taxable wage definition) are out
of scope.
