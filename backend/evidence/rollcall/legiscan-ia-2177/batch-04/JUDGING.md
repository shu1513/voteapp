# Iowa batch-04 — judging notes

Sources: the enrolled act on legis.iowa.gov, which is ground truth; the Legislative Services
Agency's explanation of the introduced bill, used only for triage because it describes the
bill as introduced and is often never rewritten; and the bill history. Sponsor material was
not used. Iowa's enrolled text draws deletions and insertions as lines at fixed coordinates,
so a plain text extraction keeps both the old and the new words; every description below was
written from the resolved markup.

## HF 571 — Medical Ethics Defense Act (House 63-27, Senate 30-16) — healthcare_affordability, yea against
A broad right for any practitioner or institution to refuse to take part in a health care
service that conflicts with its conscience, which for an institution is read from its own
governing documents, backed by immunity for a good-faith refusal from civil, criminal and
licensing consequences and a private action against anyone who interferes. The act keeps
hospitals' federal emergency-treatment duty, and the descriptions say so. The whistleblower and free speech sections run
the other way and are described, but they are not comparable in weight to the refusal right.

## HF 516 — medical residency and admissions (House 61-30, Senate 34-11) — healthcare_affordability, yea for
Eighty percent of University of Iowa medical and dental admissions reserved for Iowans or
students from Iowa colleges, priority in residencies and fellowships for the same group, and a
rural rotation offered to primary care residents. The aim throughout is keeping doctors in the
state, which is what a patient feels as access.

## HF 398 — corrections governance (House 62-29, Senate 32-15) — anti_corruption, yea against
The word struck throughout is "approval" and the words added are "advice and recommendations":
the director no longer needs the Board of Corrections to sign off on the budget, the rules for
running the institutions, superintendent appointments or a furlough program. The act also
removes a superintendent's right to be heard on written charges before removal. Less
independent oversight of a closed institution is the direction.

## HF 2724 — teaching endorsements (Senate 30-16) — public_education_quality, yea for
New endorsements in fine arts, mathematics and science for prekindergarten through grade twelve,
with a bachelor's degree required.

## SF 2218 — false Social Security numbers and identity checks (House 71-20) — immigration, yea against
New chapter 91F bars employing anyone the employer knows has given a Social Security number
that is not their own, enforced with a ten thousand dollar penalty per violation and, on a
fourth violation, a shutdown of that location until the owner and managers complete state
training. The act also tightens identity and work eligibility checks for teacher licensing and
adds citizenship requirements for certain state posts. Labeled the way West Virginia's
mandatory E-Verify regime was labeled: a restriction.
