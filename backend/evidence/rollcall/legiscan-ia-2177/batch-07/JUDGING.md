# Iowa batch-07 — judging notes

Sources: the reprint or introduced bill on legis.iowa.gov with Iowa's drawn strike and underline
lines resolved, the Legislative Services Agency explanation, floor amendments (AMDI) and the
bill history. No sponsor material. Every description is conditional ("would have") and ends
with a fate sentence derived from the dataset.

- **HF 2102** (House 58-30) immigration, yea against. Strikes driver's license tests from the
  exception to Iowa's official-English law and bars testing in another language. A language
  barrier for immigrants, E-Verify precedent on the area.
- **HF 2133** (House 69-26) public_safety_and_crime_control, yea for. Lists kratom in Schedule I
  (section 124.204). The regulation substitute H-8168 lost 44-51, which the description states.
- **HF 269** (House 63-34) civil_rights, yea against. Bars required diversity, equity, and
  inclusion or critical race theory courses and faculty requirements at the regents
  universities. Ohio SB 1 precedent: a DEI ban is civil_rights against.
- **HF 2487** (House 62-27) civil_rights, yea against. Attorney general subpoenas and special
  counsel to enforce HF 856 and chapter 261J, plus a regents review of required courses.
- **HF 2488** (House 57-34) civil_rights, yea against. Bars DEI offices at private colleges in
  the Iowa Tuition Grant program; loss of grant eligibility after 30 days is the sanction.
- **HF 2513** (reprint, House 68-27) immigration, yea against. Hiring ban on H-1B holders from
  foreign-adversary and terrorism-sponsor countries at all Iowa colleges.
- **HF 2529** (House 57-33) corporate_accountability, yea for. Right to repair for diesel
  exhaust fluid systems on farm equipment; unfair trade practice; waivers void.
- **HF 2557** (House 65-31) civil_rights, yea against. Raising a child "consistent with the
  child's sex", including refusing gender transition care, is not abuse or endangerment and
  cannot count against foster, adoptive, or custodial parents.
- **HF 2584** (reprint, House 70-21) public_safety_and_crime_control, yea for. 300-foot
  drug-free zones around funded homeless services, one added year, no probation, $2,000 fine.
- **HF 2624** (reprint, House 64-30) public_safety_and_crime_control, yea for. DNA at arrest for
  a felony or violent aggravated misdemeanor, with automatic expungement. Review fix
  2026-09-11: the description now names the two retention exceptions the bill keeps from
  current 81.9 (a sample owed for another case; evidence in another investigation, the bill's
  own addition). 80 records rewritten; ledger `import-review-fix-rerun-report.json`.
- **HF 2718** (House 67-29) gun_control, yea against. Anti-red-flag act with a $50,000 civil
  penalty per case.
- **HF 546** (House 64-31) public_safety_and_crime_control, yea for. $100,000 minimum initial
  bond for six violent or gun crimes on a warrant; no after-hours bail schedule for them.
