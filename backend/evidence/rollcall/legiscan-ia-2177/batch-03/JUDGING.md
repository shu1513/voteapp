# Iowa batch-03 — judging notes

Sources: the enrolled act on legis.iowa.gov, which is ground truth; the Legislative Services
Agency's explanation of the introduced bill, used only for triage because it describes the
bill as introduced and is often never rewritten; and the bill history. Sponsor material was
not used. Iowa's enrolled text draws deletions and insertions as lines at fixed coordinates,
so a plain text extraction keeps both the old and the new words; every description below was
written from the resolved markup.

## HF 295 — Accreditation Autonomy Act (House 65-32, Senate 32-15) — public_education_quality, yea against
An accreditor may not take adverse action against an Iowa public college for obeying state law
or refusing to break it, and the college may sue for damages and fees. Accreditation is the main
external check on a public college, and the act limits it, so the direction on accountability is
against. Whether the accreditor was overreaching is a question of worth, not direction.

## HF 437 — Center for Intellectual Freedom (House 60-37, Senate 32-15) — public_education_quality, yea for
A single-purpose act creating an academic unit with its own tenure-track faculty, a director
appointed by the regents from finalists a regents-named council submits, and an annual report
to the legislature. The
direction on teaching capacity is for. The dispute over whether the legislature should create an
academic unit is about worth.

## HF 440 — College Affordability Act (House 64-33) — cost_of_living_reduction, yea for
Thirty days' notice of a tuition increase with the regents' own memo, a final decision at a
regular meeting, a three-year degree at every regents university, an employer-paid work study
program, and a study of holding tuition at the first-year rate. Every strand aims at what a
degree costs. Only the House roll is on the text that became law.

## HF 785 — charter school board members (House 60-36, Senate 32-15) — public_education_quality, yea against
Small but real: the requirement that non-local board members live in Iowa is replaced by a
United States citizenship requirement, and one member may now live in another state.

## HF 2230 — teacher preparation and licensing (Senate 28-15) — public_education_quality, yea for
Clinical experience with students who have disabilities and with English learners becomes
mandatory in every approved preparation program from July 2027, and special education
applicants must finish a state course on special education law and assessment.

## SF 369 — civics test for graduation (House 60-33) — public_education_quality, yea for
The department builds a multiple-choice test from the naturalization civics questions, updates
it after each presidential election, and schools must see each student pass it once before
graduating, beginning with the 2026-2027 school year.

## SF 175 — fetal development in the curriculum (House 60-31, Senate 33-16) — womens_reproductive_rights, yea against
Section 5 is the operative one: in grades five and six the human growth and development unit
must cover development inside the womb and must include a high-definition ultrasound video
showing the brain, heart and other organs. The standards flow into the required high school
health unit and reach charter and innovation zone schools.

## SF 2399 — bail (House 72-21) — public_safety_and_crime_control, yea for
A magistrate may not go below the uniform bond schedule or release without bail unless the
reason is written down, and release on personal recognizance is limited to nonviolent, nondrug
simple or serious misdemeanors. The "significant weight" sentence in the introduced bill is
not in the act and was removed from the descriptions in review. The judicial council must
revise the schedule.
