## Scope notes

- HB 4491 is injury pay under the Public Employee Disability Act: the scope
  rules count workers' compensation and injury pay as labor, and the change only
  widens it.
- SB 453 changes the Illinois Public Labor Relations Act by striking "with fewer
  than 35 employees", so the initial-agreement timetable (bargaining within 10
  days, mediation after 90) reaches every newly certified unit other than peace
  officers, firefighters and security employees.
- SB 2914 is a long school code bill; its only marked additions are the teacher
  written-warning procedure, and the description covers only those.
