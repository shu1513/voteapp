## HB 3046
New section 36t of the State Universities Civil Service Act: nothing in the act
or its rules prevents parties to a collective bargaining agreement subject to
the Illinois Educational Labor Relations Act from agreeing to a provision that
enhances employee rights. That is the whole bill.

## HB 3309
Section 3(c) of the Illinois Educational Labor Relations Act already requires
employers to give exclusive representatives reasonable access to, and
information about, the employees they represent. The bill adds that employers
must also give the "State labor organization" (the statewide organization the
exclusive representative is affiliated with) a copy of that information.

## HB 4491
Section 1(b) of the Public Employee Disability Act: an eligible employee
(corrections and Prisoner Review Board staff, Human Services staff in penal and
mental health facilities, and full-time police, firefighters and paramedics)
injured in the line of duty is paid "on the same gross pay basis, inclusive of
all pensionable salary", with continued accrual of sick leave, vacation and
pension service credits, for up to one year per injury. The corrections and
facility staff limit (injury must result from violence by inmates or residents)
is unchanged. The bill also replaces gendered pronouns.

## SB 453
Section 7 of the Illinois Public Labor Relations Act, as described in PLAN.md.

## SB 2914
Section 24-12 of the School Code: the written warning must specify the
misconduct to be remedied; a board may still treat it as part of a pattern, but
later action must be reasonably related to it; the teacher may respond in person
or in writing before the board votes; and the teacher or the exclusive
representative may take it to binding arbitration within 10 days, under the
contract's procedure or through the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service
or the American Arbitration Association, with costs split.
