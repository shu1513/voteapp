# Maryland 2026 batch-01 — selection

**Five measures / ten final rolls.** This batch uses the full LegiScan session
2240 survey, then Maryland's official Department of Legislative Services (DLS)
Fiscal and Policy Notes for the enrolled text.

## Gate

Each selected measure passed five checks:

1. A divided final floor vote: at least one nay and the smaller side is at
   least one-quarter of the larger side.
2. Enacted: the official bill history records a chapter.
3. A plain, statewide subject that fits one research area.
4. One final roll per measure and chamber. When a chamber concurred in
   amendments, the later third-reading roll is used.
5. A defensible label direction. A no vote gets a stance only when it directly
   rejects the research area's mechanism.

HB 1076, HB 103, SB 656, and SB 791 each had an earlier same-chamber floor
vote. This batch keeps the later post-amendment or post-concurrence vote. HB
115 was passed without a later amendment, so both chamber rolls are on its
enacted text.

## Cross-filed twins

Exactly one enacted twin is selected per pair. HB 1076 is selected rather than
SB 532 (Chapters 674/675); HB 115 rather than SB 241 (428/427); and SB 656
rather than HB 1533 (283/284). SB 791 is selected; its designated cross-file,
HB 1575, did not become law. HB 103 has no designated cross-file.
