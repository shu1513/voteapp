# Maryland 2026 batch-05 — selection

**43 measures / 62 final rolls**, the judged half of this session's full
disposition sweep and the last Maryland batch. Every remaining
divided-and-enacted subject in session 2240 was triaged; the 77 not judged are
listed with reasons in `../dispositions/DISPOSITIONS.md`.

## Gate

The same five checks as every Maryland batch: a divided floor vote, enacted
with a chapter, a plain statewide subject fitting one research area, one final
roll per chamber, and a label direction a no vote can honestly carry.

## Validation done before any description was written

- **Cross-filed twins** collapsed with LegiScan's `sasts` field, normalized for
  its missing space, and the judged set audited against every measure already
  approved in batches 01-03. No twin is judged twice. SB 325 and SB 346 were
  deferred on this basis, being the twins of HB 548 here and of HB 351 in
  batch-02.
- **Superseded rolls**: all 62 rolls checked for a later same-chamber vote on
  the same measure. HB 561 was dropped during that check because its only
  divided roll is superseded by a later non-divided vote (118-13).
- **Same-day peers**: none in this batch.
- **Version check**: each chaptered PDF suffix compared with its fiscal note's
  version stamp.
- **SB 187 and SB 322 became law without the Governor's signature** under
  Article II, Section 17(c). Their tally sentences say so rather than claiming
  they were signed.
