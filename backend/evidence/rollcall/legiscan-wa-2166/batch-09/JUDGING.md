# Washington batch-09 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the **Final Bill Report** on leg.wa.gov, "Synopsis as Enacted".

### Senate Bill 5217 — Healthy Starts Act, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Paid lactation breaks at the regular rate, paid travel time where the only private place
is a bathroom, no forced use of paid leave, coverage of every employer with one or more
workers including nonprofit religious groups, postpartum scheduling flexibility, Labor and
Industries enforcement plus a private right of action, and a jury duty excuse. Takes
effect 1 January 2027. House-only.

### Senate Bill 5284 — packaging and paper products, `environment_and_public_health` yes = for, no = null

Extended producer responsibility for packaging and paper: producers join a producer
responsibility organization, register with Ecology, and may not sell covered products
unregistered from 1 March 2029; the organization sets member fees and files a plan.
Exemptions for governments, nonprofits, small farms and listed product classes are carried
in brief.

### Senate Bill 5313 — lease terms, `corporate_accountability` yes = for, no = null

Voids lease terms that waive a tenant's right to sue (including class actions), that
impose nondisclosure about rent and deal terms, or that require arbitration unless the
landlord pays it all and it is notarized. The duties bind the landlord as a business
operator, following the campaign rule for private operators.

### Senate Bill 5463 — self-insurers' good faith duty, `corporate_accountability` yes = for, no = null

Extends the good faith and fair dealing duty from self-insured cities and private
firefighter employers to every self-insurer and third-party administrator, with mandatory
probation after two violations in three years and loss of self-insurance for later ones.
The duty binds employers and claims firms, not workers, so the area is
`corporate_accountability`.

### Senate Bill 5486 — captioning, `civil_rights` yes = for, no = null

Closed captioning devices at every captioned showing, set open-caption showings for
companies with five or more theaters, a request option for smaller ones, advertising and
record rules. Access for deaf and hard of hearing people is equal treatment. Senate-only.

### Senate Bill 5525 — layoff notice, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Sixty days' notice before a closing or a mass layoff of 50 or more full-time workers, by
employers of 50 or more; content rules; exceptions for unforeseeable events, disasters and
project-limited construction work; back pay and benefits for up to 60 days on violation.

### Senate Bill 5557 — emergency care, `womens_reproductive_rights` yes = for, no = null

Hospitals must give pregnant patients with emergency conditions standard-of-care treatment,
including termination when that is the standard, or transfer with consent, and may not rank
the pregnancy above the patient's health without consent. The listed conditions are
carried. The title says it codifies emergency rules, so the description says so.
Senate-only.

### Senate Bill 5632 — shield law, `womens_reproductive_rights` yes = for, no = null

The Background shows the 2023 Shield Law (ESHB 1469) covers reproductive and
gender-affirming care. This act defines protected assistance (money, travel, information),
extends limits on officials to agents of public agencies, and requires communication
providers to tell the Attorney General about legal process lacking the required
attestation. The first draft read at grade 12 and was rewritten.

### Senate Bill 5651 — garnishment, `corporate_accountability` yes = for, no = null

Raises the automatic bank account and securities exemption from garnishment for consumer
debt from $1,000 to $2,000, makes it permanent, indexes it from 2027, and exempts alimony
and spousal support except in support collections. The duty falls on creditors collecting
consumer debt, which the area's consumer protection language reaches.
