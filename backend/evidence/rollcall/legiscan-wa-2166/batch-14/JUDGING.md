# Washington batch-14 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the nonpartisan Senate Bill Report **as passed Senate**
(`SBR APS`) on leg.wa.gov, which describes exactly the version the Senate voted on.

- **Senate Bill 5023.** Brings domestic workers under minimum wage, overtime, meal and rest
  breaks, a written agreement, and notice before termination; bans holding papers,
  surveillance of private activity, and immigration threats; state enforcement and a
  private right of action. Fair wages and worker protection.
- **Senate Bill 5061.** Public works contracts must adjust minimum wages each year to the
  latest prevailing wage, except small works roster and residential jobs. Fair wages.
- **Senate Bill 5067.** Lowers the per se alcohol limit to 0.05 for driving, physical
  control, and boating, with a public campaign and a 2029 evaluation. Enforcement.
- **Senate Bill 5098.** Adds public buildings, children's park areas and open county fairs
  to the weapons-free list. **Concealed pistol license holders are exempt** from the new
  bans, and the description says so. Gun shows on fairgrounds are also exempt.
- **Senate Bill 5103.** The Clemency and Pardons Board must consider expedited review when
  deportation is pending, and immigration status does not affect eligibility. A more
  humane process for people facing removal.
- **Senate Bill 5123.** Adds ethnicity, homelessness, immigration or citizenship status and
  neurodivergence to public school nondiscrimination, and splits sexual orientation, gender
  expression and gender identity into three classes. Anti-discrimination.
- **Senate Bill 5215.** Covered loads of loose material on paved roads, phased in by
  2028-01-01, with public road crew exemptions. Current law allows six inches of freeboard
  instead, which the background states. Road safety.
- **Senate Bill 5280.** Cryptocurrency kiosks: $1,000 daily limit, fee cap of $5 or 5
  percent including spread, fraud warnings, receipts. Consumer protection.
- **Senate Bill 5328.** Licenses employer-integrated earned wage access with a $5 per
  advance and $10 per month fee cap, under the Consumer Protection Act; treats
  direct-to-consumer products as consumer loans. Consumer protection.
- **Senate Bill 5331.** Insurance Commissioner restitution orders with 8 percent interest,
  and fines per violation instead of a $10,000 total. Corporate accountability.
