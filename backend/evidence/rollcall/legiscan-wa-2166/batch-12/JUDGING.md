# Washington batch-12 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the **Final Bill Report** on leg.wa.gov, "Synopsis as Enacted".
The labor backfill's scope rules apply (see
`evidence/rollcall/labor-rights-backfill-2026-09-10/NOTES.md`): an act that both widens and
narrows worker protections gets no labor label. Each act below moves one way.

- **House Bill 1068.** Grants interest arbitration to Washington Management Service
  employees at the Department of Corrections. Bargaining is the area's own mechanism.
- **House Bill 1069.** Makes supplemental retirement contributions for corrections
  employees a mandatory subject of bargaining, outside interest arbitration and outside the
  health care dollar cap. Senate-only.
- **House Bill 1141.** A full organizing and bargaining framework for cannabis agricultural
  workers under the Public Employment Relations Commission: an interference ban,
  elections at 30 percent, card check at a majority with no incumbent, and good faith
  bargaining.
- **House Bill 1213.** Paid family and medical leave: four-hour minimum claims, job
  protection phased down to employers of 8, a 180-day tenure test instead of hours, health
  coverage kept during protected leave, and a small employer grant. The act also sets
  maximum protection periods and a stacking rule. Those bound an expanded right rather than
  cut an existing one, so the act moves one way.
- **House Bill 1570.** Brings nonacademic student employees at Western Washington
  University under the public employee bargaining act, with listed exclusions from scope.
- **House Bill 1821.** Adds joint labor-management committees and Taft-Hartley trusts to
  the parties who may obtain certified payroll records, for prevailing wage complaints
  only. Prevailing wage enforcement is fair wages.
- **House Bill 2471.** A contingent state private-sector bargaining law that applies only
  if federal preemption lapses or the National Labor Relations Board declines or loses
  jurisdiction. The contingency is the description's first sentence after the
  background.
- **Senate Bill 5503.** Public Employment Relations Commission procedure (early offers of
  proof, hearing dates, adverse inferences with a union privilege carve-out), plus a ban on
  requiring waiver of statutory claims to settle a grievance. The procedural changes speed
  up organizing petitions, and the waiver ban protects workers. It moves one way.
- **Senate Bill 6188.** Removes the cap that tied asbestos certification rules to federal
  minimums, and requires evidence-based rules no more burdensome than needed. Safe
  workplaces are in the area's description.
- **Senate Bill 6302.** Required investigation of suspected misclassification of finishing
  trades workers on public works, with prevailing wage liability and insurance referrals.
  Misclassification is defined in the first sentence so the reader knows what is being
  enforced. Senate-only.
