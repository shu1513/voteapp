## Why these were reopened

The survey dropped 11 Washington measures with the reason "no labor or union research
area exists". The `labor_rights` area now exists (migration 277, merged 2026-09-10), so that
reason is no longer true. All 11 were re-read:
- **8 are judged here**: House Bills 1068, 1069, 1141, 1570, 1821 and 2471, and Senate
  Bills 5503 and 6302. Their worklist rows change from `dropped` to `batch-12`.
- **3 stay dropped, with new reasons**: House Bill 1264 (how the ferry salary survey is
  done; administrative), Senate Bill 5459 (call center relocation notice; job protection
  from offshoring is not in the area's description), and Senate Bill 5790 (which inflation
  index sets college staff raises; fiscal machinery).

House Bill 1213 and Senate Bill 6188 were still-open candidates and join the batch.

House Bill 1069 is **Senate-only**: its last House vote, on the Senate's changes, was 78-17
and not divided. Senate Bill 6302 is Senate-only (House 92-4). House Bill 2471 only takes
effect if federal labor law stops covering private workers, and its description says so
first.
