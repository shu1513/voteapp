# Washington batch-06 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the **Final Bill Report** on leg.wa.gov, "Synopsis as Enacted",
written by non-partisan legislative staff with no sponsor statement of intent. Where the
Summary alone could not show what changed, the report's Background was read as well, and
for House Bill 1215 the session law itself.

## Per measure

### House Bill 1177 — child welfare housing, `social_programs_and_welfare` yes = for, no = null

The Child Welfare Housing Assistance Program gives vouchers, rent help and services to
families whose housing is the barrier to keeping or reuniting a child. The act requires
the department to serve families on the waiting list with the aim of ending it, subject
to appropriated funds, lets help continue after the child welfare case closes, and adds
rules and reporting. "Within the money the Legislature provides" is carried because it is
the limit on the duty.

### House Bill 1215 — living will form, `womens_reproductive_rights` yes = for, no = null

The session law shows the deleted line in double parentheses: "If I have been diagnosed
as pregnant and that diagnosis is known to my physician, this directive shall have no force
or effect during the course of my pregnancy." The act removes it from the statutory model
form. The area's description includes individual bodily autonomy, and the line overrode a
pregnant person's own written choice about life support. The description says "suggested
form" because the form is a model, not a mandate.

### House Bill 1308 — personnel files, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Defines the personnel file, requires a private employer to provide it free within 21 days
and a signed discharge statement on a former worker's written request, and creates a
private right of action with a five-day notice and statutory damages of $250, $500 or
$1,000 by lateness. Public employers answer under the Public Records Act.

### House Bill 1332 — ride-hailing drivers, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Replaces the price multiplier disclosure with trip-level incentive disclosure, adds a
24-month downloadable receipt record within three days, requires product-class vehicle
rules to be published with 120 days' notice of change, and reinstates vehicles dropped
for age or model in the prior 12 months. Washington treats these drivers as workers under
its 2022 transportation network company law, so the area is `labor_rights`. One section
took effect a year later, so the description says "most of it".

### House Bill 1524 — isolated workers, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

The Background shows panic buttons and training were already required; the act redefines
who is isolated, sets panic button standards, requires records, and gives Labor and
Industries enforcement with $1,000 per willful violation and $2,000 to $10,000 per repeat
willful violation. The description says "already required" so the change is not
overstated. Senate-only (House 86-10).

### House Bill 1531 — public health measures, `environment_and_public_health` yes = for, no = null

Declares that responses to communicable disease follow the best available science,
requires officials to implement and promote such measures within available resources, and
voids any state or local rule that bans them. The act's own disclaimer, that it creates no
individual vaccine requirement, is carried, because it is what opponents would ask first.

### House Bill 1644 — child labor, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Extends citations and penalties to agriculture, sets penalty floors by violation type
(including $15,000 for serious harm and $71,000 for death, doubled for willful or repeat),
requires permit revocation for 12 months after serious harm or death, requires a worksite
safety consultation before a student-learner variance, and indexes penalties from 2027.
The description names the lowest and the two highest figures only.

### House Bill 1696 — covenant homeownership, `housing_affordability` yes = for, no = null

Raises the income ceiling from 100 to 120 percent of county median and allows full loan
forgiveness after five years for buyers at or below 80 percent. The one-line history of
racially restrictive covenants is carried because the program means nothing without it,
and it is stated as the Background states it.

## Dropped after reading

Every measure read in this session that did not become a batch keep has its reason in the
survey worklist. The main classes: fiscal and tax machinery (the standing rule), measures
that run both ways inside one area (HB 1119, HB 1293, HB 1392, HB 2219, SB 5143), study
or reporting only, and subjects no research area reaches.

**House Bill 1788** (workers' compensation benefit rates) was a planned keep and was
dropped. The act is void unless the budget funded it, and the codified statute on the
Legislature's own site still shows the old percentages in September 2026, after the act's
effective date. The change could not be confirmed.
