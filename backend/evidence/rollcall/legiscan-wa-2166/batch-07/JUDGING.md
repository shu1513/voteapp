# Washington batch-07 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the **Final Bill Report** on leg.wa.gov, "Synopsis as Enacted",
written by non-partisan legislative staff. The Background was read wherever the Summary
alone could not show what changed.

### House Bill 1974 — land banks, `housing_affordability` yes = for, no = null

Lets a city or county authorize a public corporation, housing authority or nonprofit to
run a land bank that acquires, holds and transfers property for affordable housing, may
sell below market for affordability commitments, must deed-restrict at least half of what
it transfers for 30 years (80 percent of median for rentals, 120 for ownership), gets
priority on tax-foreclosed property, and receives tax exemptions. The bar on a nonprofit
land bank building housing itself is carried because it limits what the law creates.

### House Bill 2039 — child support pass-through delay, `social_programs_and_welfare` yes = **against**, no = null

The Background shows a 2024 act (ESHB 1652) would have passed all current child support to
families on cash assistance from 1 January 2026 and stopped counting it as income. This act
postpones both to 1 July 2029. The effect of a yes vote is that families receiving aid keep
less of the support paid for their children for three and a half more years, which runs
against the area's description. Nay is null: a no vote is a vote for the earlier date, not
a separate policy.

### House Bill 2040 — disability aid repayment delay, `social_programs_and_welfare` yes = **against**, no = null

A 2023 act ended the state's recovery of Aged, Blind, or Disabled cash aid later duplicated
by federal SSI, effective 1 October 2025. This act moves that date to 1 October 2028, so
recovery continues three more years. Same reasoning as House Bill 2039. The judged House
roll is the same-day reconsideration vote; see PLAN.md.

### House Bill 2165 — false identification as a peace officer, `public_safety_and_crime_control` yes = for, no = null

Creates a gross misdemeanor for claiming or giving the impression of being a state, local or
federal peace officer when a reasonable person would believe it, defines insignia, and
exempts retirees within agency policy, commemorative items, and art, satire and news. The
old impersonation means it replaces is named so the reader does not think the conduct was
legal before.

### House Bill 2242 — preventive services, `healthcare_affordability` yes = for, no = null

Fixes required preventive coverage to federal recommendations as of 30 June 2025, lets the
Insurance Commissioner update them only in a way at least as favorable, removes plans'
authority to drop a service when a federal list removes it, and ties required vaccine
coverage to the state Department of Health's recommendations instead of the federal
advisory committee. The area is `healthcare_affordability` because the operative content is
coverage without cost-sharing. The vaccine authority shift is described plainly because it
is what opponents contested.

### House Bill 2355 — domestic workers, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Minimum wage and overtime, written agreements, two or four weeks' notice or severance,
bars on seizing documents and on surveillance in private spaces, anti-retaliation, and
Labor and Industries enforcement, for people paid four or more hours a month for work in
private homes. Takes effect 1 July 2027, so the description says "takes effect".

### House Bill 2411 — shared leave, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Adds hate crime victims and immigration enforcement hardship to the state employee shared
leave program. The work-authorization condition and the confidentiality rule are both
carried, because they bound who benefits.

### House Bill 2548 — health care material change notices, `corporate_accountability` yes = for, no = null

Adds changes of majority ownership or control, majority asset sales including
sale-leasebacks, and nonprofit-to-for-profit conversions to the transactions that need 60
days' notice to the Attorney General, adds a waiting period after an information request,
quarterly public posting, and a filing fee. The duties bind private health care entities.
