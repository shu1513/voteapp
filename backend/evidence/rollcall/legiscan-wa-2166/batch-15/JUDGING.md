# Washington batch-15 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the nonpartisan Senate Bill Report **as passed Senate**
(`SBR APS`), checked against the passed version's text where a detail mattered.

- **Senate Bill 5360.** Replaces the criminal penalties in the Water Pollution Control,
  Clean Air and Hazardous Waste Management Acts with three tiers: class B felony (knowing,
  with known imminent danger), class C felony (knowing), gross misdemeanor (criminal
  negligence). Construction stormwater sediment exempt. Stronger environmental enforcement.
- **Senate Bill 5466.** Creates the Washington Electric Transmission Authority with a
  nine-member board and a narrow environmental review exemption for rebuilding existing
  lines above 115 kilovolts. Grid infrastructure.
- **Senate Bill 5469.** Bans landlord coordination services that pool data to recommend
  rents for multiple landlords; public-data estimates excepted; Attorney General and
  private enforcement. Keeps rents set by competition.
- **Senate Bill 5490.** Jail search rules for transgender and intersex people at or above
  the federal Prison Rape Elimination Act, no searches to learn genital status, and limits
  on cross-gender searches and viewing. Fair treatment in custody.
- **Senate Bill 5496.** Caps business entities at 100 single-family homes and bars
  investment entities from buying any, with listed exceptions, a $100,000 penalty, and a
  forced sale. More homes left to owner-occupant buyers.
- **Senate Bill 5576.** Local option excise tax of up to 4 percent on platform short-term
  rentals, from 2026-04-01, by council vote, spent on affordable housing and rent help.
  Funds housing programs.
- **Senate Bill 5581.** Integrates shared-use paths into highway planning and design,
  allows fuel tax spending on them, and requires crossing signs. Transportation
  infrastructure.
- **Senate Bill 5647.** Extends the real estate excise tax exemption from self-help housing
  to any ownership housing an affordable homeownership facilitator sells to a low-income
  household, until 2030-01-01. Lowers the cost of affordable homes.
- **Senate Bill 5701.** The Senate version of the certified payroll access that became law
  in 2025 as House Bill 1821 (batch-12). Fair wages enforcement.
- **Senate Bill 5708.** Age estimation for apps with addictive feeds, a bar on reusing that
  data, and no addictive feeds or timed push notifications to minors without parental
  consent. Children's personal data.
- **Senate Bill 5797.** A $5 per $1,000 annual tax on publicly traded stocks, bonds, ETFs
  and mutual funds above $50 million per resident, with broad exemptions. A tax on the very
  largest holdings, which narrows wealth gaps.
- **Senate Bill 5800.** Authorizes up to $7 billion in highway bonds and raises the State
  Route 520 bond limit by $500 million. Transportation infrastructure.
