# Washington batch-10 — how each measure was judged

Source for every measure: the **Final Bill Report** on leg.wa.gov, "Synopsis as Enacted".
One neutral official source beyond the reports was used: the Attorney General's news
release on the Senate Bill 5375 injunction.

### Senate Bill 5375 — clergy reporting, `public_safety_and_crime_control` yes = for, no = null

Adds clergy, broadly defined, to mandatory reporters of child abuse and neglect, and makes
clergy the one group that may not withhold a report because the knowledge came from a
privileged communication. Protecting children through reporting is prevention, which the
area names. The confession injunction is stated because a reader would otherwise think the
whole duty is in force. The carve-out is not Catholic-only: the order covers any privileged
communication under RCW 5.60.060(3), so the text says "clergy of any faith" and
"confession or its equivalent".

### Senate Bill 5662 — connection charge waivers, `housing_affordability` yes = for, no = null

In an eastern Washington county over 500,000 people, lets utilities waive connection
charges for shelter and affordable housing without backfilling the money, if a covenant
limits the use and requires payment on conversion. House-only.

### Senate Bill 5686 — association dues mediation, `housing_affordability` yes = for, no = null

Opens foreclosure mediation to condominium and homeowner association owners behind on
assessments, with a meet-and-confer step, notice within 30 days, a 15-day wait, and caps on
early collection charges. Keeping owners in their homes is the area's cost-burden goal.

### Senate Bill 5714 — bail agents, `immigration` yes = for, no = null

Makes it unprofessional conduct for a bail recovery agent to use the position to enforce a
civil immigration warrant, or for an agent to share a defendant's immigration status
outside the business. House-only.

### Senate Bill 5847 — injured workers, `labor_rights` yes = for, no = null

Notice of the right to choose a provider, a ban on coercing the choice, investigation and
penalties, and an out-of-network option when no network provider is within 25 miles. Safe
workplaces and their aftermath sit in the area's description.

### Senate Bill 5855 — police masks, `public_safety_and_crime_control` yes = for, no = null

Bars identity-concealing face coverings for state and federal officers dealing with the
public, with exceptions, and gives a person detained by an unlawfully masked officer a
right to sue. The area's description names accountability in policing. Federal officers
are named because the act names them.

### Senate Bill 5892 — voter registration data, `election_integrity` yes = for, no = null

Routes all records requests for statewide list data to the Secretary of State, makes
knowing disclosure a class C felony, and keeps identifying numbers and full birth dates
confidential. Securing the voter list is the area's "secure" element. It does not change
who may vote, so the voter-access rule does not apply.

### Senate Bill 5925 — civil investigative demands, `civil_rights` yes = for, no = null

Gives the Attorney General pre-suit investigative demands for constitutional, wage,
anti-discrimination, immigration-cooperation and jail law violations, with limits (no use
against federal agencies for official acts, none in criminal matters). Most of the listed
laws are anti-discrimination or equal treatment laws, which the area names.

### Senate Bill 5981 — 340B, `healthcare_affordability` yes = for, no = null

Bars drug makers from restricting 340B drug delivery to covered entities and contract
pharmacies or demanding data as a condition, with private and Attorney General enforcement
and fines up to $5,000 a day, and requires covered entities to report savings and their
use. The discount program exists to lower costs for low-income patients.

### Senate Bill 6182 — Abortion Savings Program, `womens_reproductive_rights` yes = for, no = null

Grants, subject to appropriation, to keep abortion care available for people without
sufficient resources, with at least 85 percent of funds as grants and confidentiality for
staff and practitioners.
