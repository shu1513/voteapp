# New Mexico roll-call evidence — 2025 1st Special Session (LegiScan session 2227)

New Mexico's legislature met for two days in October 2025. Small, but two of its measures matter:
one widened the state's health care affordability fund, and one moved control of the state's vaccine
rules away from a federal advisory committee. The 2026 regular session then repealed part of the
second one, which is House Bill 156 in `../legiscan-nm-2251/`.

## Layout

Same as the other New Mexico sessions: `crosswalk.json`, the people snapshot, `survey/` with the
survey report and the dispositioned worklist, and `batch-01/` with the judgments, the roll evidence
and the import ledgers.

## What the survey found

11 bills, 10 roll calls, 112 people. Five `House Final Passage` and five `Senate Final Passage`, all
on plain bills. No committee votes, no parse errors, no roll missing its member list.

## The pool

Five rolls are divided and enacted: three House and two Senate. The two Senate rolls fan out to
nobody, because no New Mexico Senate seat is on the 2026 ballot. Of the three House rolls, two are
imported and one is the session's general appropriations act, excluded like every other budget bill
in this campaign.

## The second special session is not here

LegiScan session 2232, held that December, holds two bills and two roll calls and neither roll is
divided. It can never produce a record, so it is not registered in the config and has no evidence
directory.

## Status

**2 measures, 120 records, 62 candidates, 85 tags, on the local database only. Production holds no
New Mexico roll-call records.**
