# New Mexico 2025 first special session, batch-01 — selection

**2 measures, 2 House roll calls, 120 records across 62 candidates.**

The whole session is five divided-and-enacted rolls. Two Senate rolls fan out to nobody, because no
New Mexico Senate seat is on the 2026 ballot. Of the three House rolls, two are imported and one is
the session's general appropriations act, excluded like every budget bill in this campaign.

## The 2

| Measure | Roll | Vote | Area, and which way a yes vote points |
|---|---|---|---|
| House Bill 2 | 1603193 | 49-13 | healthcare affordability, for |
| Senate Bill 3 | 1603536 | 43-26 | environment and public health, for |

Both labels set the nay side explicitly to null.

Senate Bill 3 is worth reading beside House Bill 156 of the 2026 regular session. This act moved
control of New Mexico's vaccine rules from a federal advisory committee to the state Department of
Health, and it carried a delayed reversion that would have handed that control back on July 1, 2026.
House Bill 156 repealed the reversion. A voter looking at both records sees the same direction twice.

## Excluded

**House Bill 1** (43-24), the session's general appropriations act. No research area carries an
honest direction on a vote to fund the government.
