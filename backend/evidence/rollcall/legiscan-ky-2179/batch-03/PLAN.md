## Why four of the seven carry no stance

Each of these four has two real strands that pull different ways, which is the
same shape as HB 90 and HB 695 in batch-01 and HB 2 and HB 424 in batch-02. All
four are consequential and name a subject a reader recognizes, so they are
imported with the `general` label and no stance rather than dropped.

- **HB 241** pairs storm-day relief for school districts, which almost nobody
  opposes, with a statewide cap on out-of-district enrollment in public online
  school programs and an end to new out-of-district enrollment after June 2028.
  The cap protects home districts and limits family choice at the same time.
- **HB 606** creates 500-job development sites that can be organized into a
  taxing district. The board that levies the property tax and the wage tax is
  appointed rather than elected, and the Act waives the voter signature
  threshold that normally applies, but the fee is capped, can never rise, and
  expires after twenty years.
- **HB 664** pairs work zone speed cameras, which only a peace officer standing
  in the zone may act on and whose images may not be shown to anyone but the
  driver, with lowering from 16 to 15 the age at which a child charged with a
  motor vehicle offense goes to adult District Court. The safety strand and the
  juvenile justice strand sit in different research areas and point different
  ways; tagging a yes vote against civil rights because of the second half
  would misdescribe the first half.
- **SB 28** creates a farm and agribusiness incentive program with grants and
  forgivable loans, which reads as new subsidy spending, but the same Act
  requires an annual public report on every award and lets the board claw money
  back when a company breaks its agreement. It also moves the appointment power
  to the elected Commissioner of Agriculture, which is what the Governor's veto
  message was about.

## The one measure dropped

**HB 618** is alcoholic beverage licensing mechanics: buying stock directly from
a retailer, a special temporary auction license for licensed auctioneers, and
which licenses may be held together. There is no research area for it and no
direction to name, so it fails filter 3 and filter 5 both.
