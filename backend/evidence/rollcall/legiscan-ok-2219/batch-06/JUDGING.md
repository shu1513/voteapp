# How batch 06 was judged

All twelve acts, the six imported and the six dropped, were read from the enrolled text with
the strikethrough and underline marks visible.

**SB 137, electronic monitoring.** The act adds one group to the list of inmates barred from
serving part of a sentence at home on an ankle monitor: people convicted under the section of
the motor vehicle code on causing an accident that results in great bodily injury while
driving under the influence. That reading was checked against the statute itself on the
state's official statutes site, not inferred from the cross-reference.
`public_safety_and_crime_control`, yes = for.

**SB 713, wind farm lights.** Turbines carry aircraft warning lights that usually blink all
night. The act requires a system that keeps them dark until it detects a plane, where the
federal aviation and radio regulators approve one: from 2026 for new wind farms, and at the
next rebuild or new long-term power contract for existing ones. The wind farm pays; a county
may issue bonds; a regulated utility may seek to recover its costs; wind farms wholly on land
their owner holds outright are exempt. Scored `environment_and_public_health`, yes = for,
because the effect a neighbor feels is darker nights with aviation safety kept. The competing
reading, that it adds a cost to wind power, is real but does not restrict where or whether
wind farms are built, so the no side is null.

**HB 3644, the Blake Burgess Act.** Hospitals with an emergency room and outpatient surgery
centers must adopt evidence-based policies to assess and treat deep vein blood clots and train
unlicensed clinical staff on them every year. The State Department of Health must build a
statewide registry, with hospital reporting from July 2027, and assisted living centers must
screen new residents and give them a leaflet on the warning signs.
`environment_and_public_health`, yes = for.

**HB 3834, ibogaine trials.** The State Department of Health may contract with a developer to
run trials aimed at federal approval of ibogaine, subject to available money. The developer
must match the state dollar for dollar, run the trials in Oklahoma, plan access for uninsured
and low-income patients, and pay the state a share of any profits. A new fund holds that
money for research and programs on brain injury and addiction, and doctors may not be
disciplined only for recommending a trial. `environment_and_public_health`, yes = for.

**HB 4071, Trump Accounts.** The State Treasurer may make a one-time $250 deposit into the
federal Trump Account of an eligible child, in the order applications are verified and only as
money reaches a new fund. The act creates the fund but appropriates nothing into it, and the
description says so, so no reader takes it for a payment already funded.
`social_programs_and_welfare`, yes = for.

**HB 1727, Oklahoma's Promise.** The act opens the state's free tuition scholarship to the
children of classroom teachers with at least ten years in Oklahoma public schools, with an
income limit of 700 percent of the federal poverty level, and keeps administrators out of
the definition of a teacher. It lets students sign up as late as December 31 of their last
year of high school and bars anyone 21 or older. `public_education_quality`, yes = for.

## Things worth recording

- **Two traps in the pool itself, both caught by reading the history rather than the tally.**
  HB 4324 carries a successful override roll but never became law, because only one chamber
  overrode. HB 1427's Senate slot is a failed 17-25 vote whose real replacement is not in the
  feed. Neither is visible from the roll list alone. Every remaining measure's final status
  was then checked against the feed, and HB 4324 was the only one not enacted.
- **SB 1027 is the clearest case yet of one area pulling two ways.** Honesty rules and limits
  on who may take part both sit in `election_integrity`. The Arkansas line scores the first and
  takes no stance on the second, and with `general` barred from roll-call records, a measure
  holding both cannot be recorded honestly under one label.
