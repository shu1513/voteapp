# How batch 08 was judged

The last four measures in the Oklahoma pool, all read from the enrolled text with the
strikethrough and underline marks visible, all recorded on their Senate roll.

**SB 20, commercial drivers.** Anyone driving a commercial vehicle in Oklahoma must hold a
license from a U.S. state or territory, or a Canadian, Mexican or reciprocal foreign license
carried with a valid work visa and proof of citizenship of the issuing country. A license
Oklahoma issues to a driver who is not a U.S. resident now expires with the visa. Every
commercial driver must know enough English to talk with the public, read signs, answer
officials and keep records. Carriers are fined $3,000 a violation and the truck is held; the
driver is barred, and driving anyway is a misdemeanor.

Scored `immigration`, yes = **against**, on the campaign's existing line: measures that tie a
job to immigration or work status, such as E-Verify mandates, are recorded that way in 505
records across other states. The English rule is also a road safety rule, and the description
names it plainly, but the act as a whole decides who may drive a commercial vehicle, so it
carries the one label. The Senate roll is its adoption of the conference report, which is a
vote on the final text.

**SB 1847, the ADvantage program.** This is the Medicaid program that pays for care at home
instead of in a nursing home. It was open to people 65 and over and to adults 21 and over with
a physical disability and no intellectual disability or cognitive impairment. For adults with
a physical disability the act lowers the age to 19 and drops that bar, and it adds people aged
19 to 64 with a developmental disability but no related intellectual disability, or with a
worsening disease that once needed hospital or nursing home care. Care may also be given in a
contracted assisted living center. `healthcare_affordability`, yes = for.

**SB 54, aggravated driving under the influence.** Before the act, aggravated meant a blood or
breath alcohol level of 0.15 or more. It now also covers impaired driving combined with a
reportable crash, certain lane and turning violations, eluding police, speeding 20 over or 10
over in an active school zone, a passenger under 18, or reckless driving. The description
quotes the act's own words that the offense "shall be a felony". It sets mandatory minimum
confinement for a first and second offense, and removes the rule that a test had to be taken
within two hours of arrest. The act also rewrites the sentencing lists for repeat offenses,
including ignition interlock devices. The description leaves that out because the rewritten
lists do not make clear which items are options and which are required.
`public_safety_and_crime_control`, yes = for. This is a veto override and is described as one.

**SB 259, groundwater.** Willful failure to report water use moves from risking cancellation
to fines, penalties or suspension, and using less than a permit allows no longer shrinks the
holder's future share. Data centers may not cool with groundwater that is lost to evaporation
or discharge, and get a permit only with a low-use, recirculating system. The Water Resources
Board must act on waste, with orders to stop, rising fines, more frequent reporting or
suspension. `environment_and_public_health`, yes = for. The title also promises metering, but
the body contains no metering clause, so the description follows the body.

## The pool is closed

With this batch every one of Oklahoma's 253 closely divided, enacted measures has a written
disposition in `survey/triage.json`: 45 imported and 208 dropped. The drops that were decided
after reading an act, rather than from its description, each give the reason in plain words.
