unmarked after batch-01 and batch-02. Same five filters.

This batch is deliberately spread across areas rather than piling onto the two
that dominate Tennessee's divided votes. It carries three `for` directions,
which the first two batches had only one of between them.

## Dropped from this batch, after reading the enacted text

- **SB 1735** — reads two ways. It removes the requirement that an assurance of
  voluntary compliance under the Consumer Protection Act be in writing and filed
  with the court, which weakens oversight of settlements, and in the same act it
  adds new prohibited practices around radar jamming devices, which strengthens
  consumer protection. No single direction.
- **HB 7003** and **HB 7002**, the mid-decade congressional redistricting pair,
  stay dropped from batch-02 pending the operator's call on direction.
- School-choice financing measures stay dropped on standing precedent.

## Resolved from batch-02

**HB 910** was deferred last batch because the LegiScan summary said it
terminates the Human Rights Commission while the chaptered act still carried
commission appointment terms. Reading the act through settles it: SECTION 20
says the commission "terminates and ceases to exist at the end of the fiscal
year ending June 30, 2025", and SECTION 19 creates a civil rights enforcement
division in the office of the attorney general. The appointment language earlier
in the act is the Part 1 rewrite, not a surviving commission. Imported.

**HB 754** was deferred with it and is imported here.
