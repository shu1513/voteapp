`gun_control` appears in both directions in the same batch, as it did in
batch-01: SB 350 and SB 2459 widen access and shield gun ownership from
inquiry, SB 1747 creates a new firearm offense.

## Dropped, after reading the enacted text

- **SB 1881** — makes office of homeland security records on critical
  infrastructure confidential and exempt from public records requests. It reads
  two ways: it is a real reduction in public access, and critical-infrastructure
  security exemptions are ordinary practice with a genuine safety rationale.
  No honest single direction.
- **HB 7003** and **HB 7002**, the redistricting pair, stay dropped pending the
  operator's call.
- School-choice financing measures stay dropped on standing precedent.
