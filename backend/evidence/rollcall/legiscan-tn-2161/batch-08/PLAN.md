## Read and dropped

- **SB 669** replacing World Health Organization references with the CDC, and
  **SB 263** declaring that the WHO, UN and World Economic Forum have no
  jurisdiction in Tennessee. Both are declaratory. Neither changes a duty that
  maps to a research area with a direction.
- **SB 1723** vacating and reappointing the lottery board, **SB 1731** letting
  the state appeal an interlocutory injunction as of right, **SB 1274** moving
  environmental fee-setting to the board of water quality, oil and gas,
  **SB 1864** exempting reserve and part-time officers from private protective
  services licensing. Each is a structural or procedural change with no
  direction the text settles.
