# TN batch-07 — schools, and what the education record does not support

**7 divided floor votes across 7 enacted measures.** Same five filters.

The batch began as an education cluster. Eighteen education measures were read
and only four survived filter 5, so the batch was filled out with three others.
That result is worth stating plainly: Tennessee's divided education record is
mostly curriculum mandates and school-choice financing, and neither carries a
direction this campaign can defend.

## Education measures read and dropped under filter 5

- **SB 471** the Success Sequence Act, **SB 1890** history of communism
  standards, **HB 2395** a required civics instructional video — curriculum
  content mandates. A voter can hold a view on each, but the research areas
  available do not give any of them an honest for-or-against direction.
- **SB 1018** charter transfer fees, **SB 134** the Learning Pod Protection Act
  — school-choice financing and regulation, dropped on standing precedent.
- **HB 2194** campus institutional neutrality and free expression policies —
  reads as protecting speech and as constraining what institutions may say.
- **SB 1968** truancy referrals to juvenile court, **SB 688** state board
  subpoena power, **HB 2149** continuing a discipline investigation after
  withdrawal — each reads two ways.

## Others read and dropped

- **SB 2136** online sweepstakes gambling ban, **HB 2503** short-term
  limited-duration health plans, **HB 1133** pollution control tax credits
  extended to nuclear facilities. Each has a real constituency on both sides and
  no direction the text settles.

## Deferred

About 119 real measures remain.
