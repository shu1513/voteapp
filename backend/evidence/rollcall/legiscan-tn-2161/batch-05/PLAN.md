## Dropped, after reading the enacted text

- **SB 30** — two subjects pulling different ways in different areas. It adds
  littering or trespassing with intent to intimidate someone out of exercising a
  constitutional right to the civil rights intimidation statute, and separately
  creates a false-name-to-an-officer offense. Rather than split it across two
  labels, it is left for a later batch.
- **SB 2088** — same problem. It widens the medical examiner definition of a
  mass shooting from four deaths to four injuries, and separately authorizes
  compelled blood or urine testing on probable cause. The second is a
  `civil_rights` question and the first is not.
- **SB 221** — allowing juvenile court dispositions into pretrial bond reports
  reads as a bail measure and as an erosion of juvenile record confidentiality
  at the same time. No single honest direction.
- **SB 1883** — procedural change to how oil and gas citation appeals are heard,
  moving them to an administrative judge sitting alone with deadlines. The
  environmental direction is not established by the text.
