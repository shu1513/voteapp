# TN batch-06 — the immigration cluster

**8 divided floor votes across 7 enacted measures**, all labeled
`immigration` with `yea: against`.

This batch is deliberately single-subject. Tennessee's 114th General Assembly
passed immigration enforcement law in volume, and spreading it thinly across
batches would have understated how much of the divided floor record it is.
Batches 02, 03 and 04 already carry five such measures; these are seven more.

| measure | yea | rolls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HB 2018 sheriffs must honor immigration detainers | against | H 72-23 |
| HB 1709 citizenship or qualified immigrant status for state licenses | against | H concurrence 70-21 |
| SB 1587 commercial driving offenses | against | H 73-20 |
| SB 1464 names of immigration enforcement officers kept secret | against | H 70-19 |
| HB 2502 sales tax on money sent abroad | against | H concurrence 70-19, S 21-7 |
| SB 750 report on crime linked to people here illegally | against | H 75-20 |
| HB 1194 work authorization law extended to employers with one employee | against | S 19-13 |

HB 1194's Senate roll at 19-13 is the closest Senate vote in any Tennessee
batch.

## Dropped

**HB 1707** — courts required to cooperate with the Department of Homeland
Security, with a judge who obstructs referable to the board of judicial conduct.
Substantively one of the most significant measures in the session, and dropped
anyway. See JUDGING.md: it is a vehicle bill whose only divided rolls predate a
conference committee report that is not in the feed.
