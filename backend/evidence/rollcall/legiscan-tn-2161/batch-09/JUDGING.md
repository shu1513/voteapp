## Label reasoning

Every label uses `nay: null`.

- **HB 1271**, `civil_rights`, against. Every local governmental entity must, by
  1 January 2027, revise any ordinance, resolution, rule, policy or procedure
  referring to a person's sex or gender so that those terms mean the immutable
  characteristics fixed at birth.
- **SB 1665**, `civil_rights`, against. Tennessee's school pronoun law is
  widened from "names and pronouns" to "names, pronouns, and honorifics", so a
  school employee cannot be required to use a preferred honorific such as Mr. or
  Ms., as well as a preferred pronoun, where it does not match the person's
  recorded sex.
- **SB 626**, `election_integrity`, **for**. The state coordinator of elections
  may grant a county more time to finish counting provisional ballots when the
  county cannot meet its deadline, up to the third Monday after the election.
  More lawfully cast provisional ballots get counted.
- **SB 1619**, `election_integrity`, **for**. It deletes the two clauses that
  had let election offices accept private funds when the speakers of the two
  chambers, or the secretary of state, approved. That approval route is closed
  outright, which completes the ban SB 222 of the 2023-2024 Georgia session and
  Tennessee's own earlier law aimed at. It also requires an administrator to
  give a rejected registrant the reason and to record it, and keeps purged
  registration files public for two years.
