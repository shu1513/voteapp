## What the remaining 70 measures are

Read or classified and left out, with the reason:

- **Education, about 25 measures** — charter school replication, authorizing
  bodies, virtual and adult charter schools, TISA formula and committee
  mechanics, accountability reporting, testing options, the school turnaround
  pilot, teacher bonus funding. Either school-choice financing, which has no
  defensible direction on standing precedent, or administrative machinery.
- **Curriculum mandates** — the Success Sequence Act, history-of-communism
  standards, the Charlie Kirk American Heritage Act on the positive impact of
  religion, a required civics instructional video. A voter can hold a view on
  each; the research areas cannot give any of them an honest direction.
- **Local bills in general-law clothing** — SB 2102 reaches exactly one
  municipal electric utility through a 485,000 population bracket, SB 734 a
  Davidson County hotel tax, HB 803 a Hamilton County sewerage exemption.
  Filter 3.
- **Structural and procedural** — vacating and reappointing the lottery board,
  the airport authority boards, insurance committee membership; commission
  restructuring in the Less is More Act; three-judge panels; state appeal
  rights; bondsmen, utilities, chiropractors and professions revisions.
- **Declaratory and symbolic** — WHO, UN and World Economic Forum have no
  jurisdiction; replacing WHO references with the CDC; Law Day; Celebration of
  Life, Liberty and the Pursuit of Happiness months; a resolution urging
  Congress to pass the Fair Tax Act.
- **Genuinely two-way on their own text** — SB 1847 lowering the standard for
  deadly force to protect property, SB 1741 the Charlie Kirk Act requiring
  Chicago-Principles expression policies, SB 1748 suspending commercial licenses
  over English proficiency, SB 217 removing camping property from highway
  rights of way, SB 2641 pension proxy advisors, HB 1376 hemp cannabinoid
  regulation, HB 2503 short-term health plans, HB 1133 nuclear pollution credits,
  SB 7 historic preservation limits.
- **56 ceremonial joint resolutions** honoring individuals, excluded on sight.
