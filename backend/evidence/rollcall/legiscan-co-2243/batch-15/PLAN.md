## Three votes where the yes side lost

- **HB 1137** lost the House 26-35.
- **HB 1245** lost the House 27-37.
- **SB 48**'s Senate agreed the House amendments 18-17 and then rejected the
  bill 11-24 on the vote to pass it again.

The wording for these says the chamber rejected the bill, and the yes-side
description still records a vote to pass it, which is what those members did.

## Four measures dropped

**HB 1221** and **HB 1222** are tax expenditure adjustments; **HB 1330** is thin;
and **SB 135** is dropped because the feed does not record its fate. See
`JUDGING.md`.

## Everything here is conditional

None became law. The builder refuses any description that does not say "would
have" and any that says "the act" or "became law".

## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. SB 48's Senate roll
has its same-day concurrence peer recorded.

Twelve of the fourteen measures have only one usable chamber.
