## One measure dropped

**HB 1411** was in the shortlist and was dropped after the act was read. See
`JUDGING.md`: it cuts benefits rather than adding them, and the two sides of the
vote do not line up with a single direction.

## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. HB 1019, HB 1044 and
SB 32's Senate roll are `Amendments Repass` votes with a same-day `Concur` peer;
those peers are recorded, not judged. HB 1425, SB 6, SB 138 and the SB 178
Senate roll have same-day third-reading amendment or permission peers, also
recorded.

Nine measures have only one usable chamber. The other chamber's last vote was
not divided, so it is superseded rather than dropped.
