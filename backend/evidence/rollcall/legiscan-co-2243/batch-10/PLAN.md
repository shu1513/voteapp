## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. Four rolls are
`Amendments Repass` with a same-day `Concur` peer; SB 13 and SB 115's House
rolls have same-day third-reading amendment and permission peers. All recorded.

HB 1127, SB 35, SB 104 and SB 174 have only one usable House chamber. HB 1256
has only a Senate chamber.

**SB 115 passed the Senate 18-17** — exactly Colorado's constitutional majority
of 18 of 35, the Senate mirror of SB 121's 33-32 House vote in batch-07.
