## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. Six rolls are
`Amendments Repass` votes with a same-day `Concur` peer; SB 40 and SB 53 have
same-day third-reading amendment and permission peers. All peers are recorded on
the judgment, not judged separately.

Four measures have only one usable chamber: HB 1015, HB 1120, SB 40 and SB 53.
The other chamber's last vote was not divided, so it is superseded.
