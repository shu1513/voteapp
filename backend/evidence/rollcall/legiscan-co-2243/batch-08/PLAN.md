## Two measures dropped

**HB 1113** and **SB 116**. See `JUDGING.md`.

## Selection

HB 1038 and HB 1084 are **conference committee** measures: each chamber adopted
the conference report and repassed the bill the same day, and the repassage roll
is the one judged. The remaining rolls are third readings or
`Amendments Repass` with a same-day peer.

HB 1318, SB 152 and HB 1223's Senate roll have same-day amendment or permission
peers, all recorded.

HB 1318 and SB 152 have only one usable chamber.

## Version check

Twenty of the 22 rolls examined came back clean at once. Two — HB 1038's Senate
roll and HB 1084's Senate roll — were flagged, and both are explained in
`JUDGING.md`: the flagged later action is the **other** chamber adopting the same
conference report, not a change to the text.
