## Three 2025 measures came back — and all three changed

This is why the descriptions were written from the 2026 acts and not adapted
from last year's:

- **HB 1001** is not the 2025 faith-land bill. Its landowners are school
  districts, colleges, boards of cooperative services, housing authorities,
  transit districts and affordable-housing nonprofits — **not faith
  organizations** — and its height floor is **38 feet, not 45**.
- **HB 1134 does not cap municipal jail sentences.** The vetoed 2025 bill did.
  This one is about 48-hour hearings, the right to counsel, and flat-fee limits.
- **HB 1424 does not require fingerprint checks.** The vetoed 2025 bill did.
  This one moves the existing privately administered check from the driver every
  five years to the company every six months.

## One measure dropped from the selection

**SB 124** was in the shortlist as the "Colorado Survivor Justice Act" and was
dropped once its act was read: the enrolled text is an administrative change to
what the automated protection order notification system records. See
`JUDGING.md`.
