## Everything here is conditional

None became law. The builder refuses any description that does not say "would
have" and any that says "the act" or "became law".

## Which print was read

Every 2026 text carries the epoch date 1969-12-31, so the print in force cannot
be found by date. It is picked by position instead, and the position depends on
the chamber the bill **started** in — see `JUDGING.md`, where a real mistake in
that logic is recorded.

## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. SB 166's Senate day
carries a **second** third-reading roll at a different tally; it is acknowledged
and explained in `JUDGING.md`.

Ten of the twelve measures have only one usable chamber, which is expected: most
of these died in the second chamber's committee without a floor vote there.
