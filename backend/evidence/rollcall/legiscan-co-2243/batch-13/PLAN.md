## Everything here is conditional

None of these became law. The builder for this scope refuses any description
that does not say "would have" and refuses any that says "the act" or "became
law". The tail of each description names the chamber's tally and then says the
governor vetoed the bill.

The ground truth read for each measure is the **enrolled** print — the text sent
to the governor — not the introduced or engrossed one.

## One measure dropped

**HB 1355** was vetoed and is dropped: the act adjusts the appropriation for one
grant program.

## Four votes at the constitutional minimum

- **HB 1236** carried the House 33-32. Colorado needs 33 of 65.
- **HB 1255**, **HB 1418** and **SB 134** each carried the Senate 18-17.
  Colorado needs 18 of 35.
