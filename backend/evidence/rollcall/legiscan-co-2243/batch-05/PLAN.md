## One measure dropped

**HB 1288** was in the shortlist and dropped: the act creates a jury selection
working group, gives it a reporting deadline, and changes no rule. See
`JUDGING.md`.

## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. Six rolls are
`Amendments Repass` with a same-day `Concur` peer. Two rolls have unusual
same-day peers that are recorded and explained in `JUDGING.md`: HB 1312's House
roll has a **second** repassage roll on the same day, and SB 190's Senate roll
has a second third-reading roll with a different tally.

HB 1138 and HB 1312 have only one usable chamber.
