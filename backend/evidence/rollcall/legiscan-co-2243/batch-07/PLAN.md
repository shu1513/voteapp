## Selection

Every roll is its chamber's last floor vote and is divided. Nine rolls are
`Amendments Repass` with a same-day `Concur` peer. SB 121's Senate day carries
**two** repassage rolls and is explained in `JUDGING.md`.

SB 51 and SB 52 have only one usable chamber.

## Version check

All 22 rolls came back clean.

## A note on the area used

Colorado's roll-call work has no labor research area to use, the same gap that
cost Nevada four measures and cost SB 5 in the 2025 session. Six measures here
are worker-protection measures and are labeled **corporate accountability**,
because each one places a duty on an employer. That is honest but imperfect and
is recorded here so a later reader knows why.
