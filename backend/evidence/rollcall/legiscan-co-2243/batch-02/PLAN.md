## Selection

Every roll here is its chamber's last floor vote on the measure and is divided
(the losing side is at least a quarter of the winning side). Seven House rolls
and two Senate rolls are `Amendments Repass` votes with a same-day `Concur`
peer; those peers are recorded on the judgment, not judged separately.

HB 1007, HB 1081, HB 1111, HB 1225 and SB 3 have only one usable chamber; the
other chamber's last vote was not divided, so it is superseded, not dropped.

## Version check

All 17 rolls came back clean under the 2026 method: no text-changing action
follows the roll each chamber is judged on. Concurring in the other chamber's
amendments accepts text and is not a change.
