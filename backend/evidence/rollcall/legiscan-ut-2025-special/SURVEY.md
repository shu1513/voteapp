# Utah 2025 special sessions — surveyed, nothing to import

Two special sessions met after the 2025 General Session:

- **2025 First Special Session**, October 2025: 17 bills (LegiScan session 2228).
- **2025 Second Special Session**, December 2025: 3 bills (LegiScan session 2238).

Every bill in both sessions was signed. The survey used Utah's own bill records
(`le.utah.gov/data/2025S1/<BILL>.json` and `2025S2`), which list every floor
vote with Utah's tally. The judging used each bill's enrolled text.

## The close votes

A vote counts as close when the smaller side is at least a quarter of the
larger. Only 6 of the 20 bills had one. Each of the other 14 passed by a wide
margin, including HB 2001, which repealed 2025 HB 267. The HB 267 records
already say it was repealed.

| bill | close votes | decision | reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HB 1005 County Governance | House 57-16, Senate 15-11 | dropped: no area | Most county councils must elect members by single district instead of at-large, with a voter lawsuit and a hotel-tax hold to enforce it. This changes how officials are elected, not who may vote, and no area covers it. |
| SB 1003 Judiciary | Senate 22-7, House 58-15 | dropped: no area | The governor now appoints the chief justice, with Senate consent, for one 8-year term, instead of the justices electing one. The legal areas are barred on roll-call records. |
| SB 1011 Redistricting Standards | Senate 22-7, House 55-18 | dropped: mixed | It writes statistical fairness tests into law (an ensemble analysis, a partisan bias test and a mean-median test). It also creates a safe harbor: a map inside the ensemble bounds is presumed not partisan without clear and convincing evidence. The strands pull opposite ways. |
| SB 1012 Congressional Boundaries Designation | Senate 18-9, House 56-17 | dropped: no direction | It enacts a backup congressional map that applies only while the 2021 map is blocked by a court. Choosing a map carries no for-or-against reading in any area. |
| SB 2001 Election Amendments | Senate 22-7 | dropped: no direction | For 2026 only, it moves the U.S. House filing period to March 9-13 and lets candidates gather signatures statewide, because the district lines changed. It is procedure. |
| SB 2002 Appellate Court Jurisdiction Amendments | Senate 22-7 | dropped: no area | Election and redistricting appeals now go straight to the Utah Supreme Court and cannot be sent to the Court of Appeals. The legal areas are barred. |

The House votes on SB 2001 and SB 2002 were not close.

## What this means

No special-session vote is imported, so neither LegiScan dataset was
downloaded and no registry entry was added. Utah's roll-call work covers both
general sessions and both special sessions. The only step left is production
promotion of the general-session records.
