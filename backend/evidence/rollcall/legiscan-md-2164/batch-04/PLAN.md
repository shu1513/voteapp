# Maryland 2025 batch-04 — selection

**24 measures / 33 final rolls**, the judged half of this session's full
disposition sweep. Every remaining divided-and-enacted subject in session 2164
was triaged; the 85 not judged are listed with reasons in
`../dispositions/DISPOSITIONS.md`.

## Gate

The same five checks as batches 01-03: a divided floor vote, enacted with a
chapter, a plain statewide subject fitting one research area, one final roll
per chamber, and a label direction a no vote can honestly carry.

## Validation done before any description was written

- **Cross-filed twins** collapsed using LegiScan's `sasts` field, normalized for
  its missing space (`SB225`), and the judged set audited against every measure
  already approved in batches 01-03. No twin is judged twice.
- **Superseded rolls**: all 33 rolls checked against the database for a later
  same-chamber vote on the same measure. Three measures — HB 107, HB 208 and
  HB 325 — were dropped from this batch during that check because their only
  divided roll is superseded by a later non-divided vote, the same error caught
  on SB 255 in batch-02.
- **Same-day peers**: one case. SB 93's House roll 1547104 shares April 7 with
  roll 1546800, so its judgment carries `acknowledge_later_rolls`.
- **Version check**: each measure's chaptered PDF suffix was compared with its
  fiscal note's version stamp.
- **HB 1294 became law without the Governor's signature** under Article II,
  Section 17(c), as Chapter 847. Its tally sentence says so.
