# Maryland 2025 batch-02 — selection

**Ten measures / nineteen final rolls**, drawn from LegiScan session 2164 (the
2025 Regular Session). Descriptions come from the official Department of
Legislative Services (DLS) Fiscal and Policy Note for the version that became
law, and the official bill history supplies each chapter number.

## Gate

Every selected measure passed the same five checks batch-01 used:

1. A divided floor vote: at least one nay, and the smaller side is at least a
   quarter of the larger side.
2. Enacted: the official bill history records a chapter.
3. A plain, statewide subject that fits one research area.
4. One final roll per measure per chamber. Where a chamber voted again after
   the other chamber amended the bill, the later roll is the one used.
5. A defensible label direction. A no vote is given a stance only when it
   directly rejects the research area's own mechanism.

## Pool this batch was drawn from

Session 2164 holds **320 divided-and-enacted rolls on 176 measures**.
Batch-01 judged 10 of them. This batch judges 10 more, leaving **156 measures**
for later batches.

## Which text each roll voted on

The chaptered PDF file name is the mechanical version check. A `…t.pdf`
chapter means the first chamber's passed text became law unchanged; a
`…e.pdf` chapter means the second chamber amended it and the enrolled text
became law.

- Chaptered on the `t` text, so both chamber rolls are on the enacted text:
  HB 390, HB 1085, HB 324, HB 783, SB 432, HB 765, HB 1315.
- Chaptered on the `e` text, so this batch uses the later post-amendment roll
  in each chamber: HB 1473 (both rolls April 7), HB 861 (Senate April 4 on the
  amended text, House April 7 agreeing to it), SB 608 (Senate April 7 agreeing
  to the House amendments).

SB 608 contributes only one roll. Its two House votes were 128-10 and 128-11,
which are not divided, so they fall outside the gate.

## Cross-filed twins

Maryland chapters both halves of a cross-filed pair, so exactly one twin is
judged. HB 390 is used rather than SB 327, and HB 324 rather than SB 293. The
twins of batch-01's measures (SB 356, SB 357, SB 68, SB 685, HB 930) stay out
for the same reason.

## Deliberately not selected

- **HB 1036 / SB 931, Renewable Energy Certainty Act** — a genuine omnibus
  (state siting rules, rooftop solar licensing, community solar, agrivoltaics,
  studies). Its limits on local zoning authority run against the same area a
  yes vote would serve, so no single honest direction fits.
- **SB 894, cannabis reinvestment fund** — mostly fund governance, and it
  repeals some reporting duties, so the direction is not clean.
- **HB 502, disability employment office** — establishes an office and an
  initiative; the direction is too thin to carry a stance.
- Budget bills, speed-camera bills and local-jurisdiction bills, as in
  batch-01.
