## SB 767
Read from Chapter 132 of 2025 (`ls_text.py`). New section 2-318(h) of the Courts
and Judicial Proceedings Article gives full-time deputy sheriffs and correctional
deputies at the rank of major and below the right to organize and bargain with
the County Commissioners and the Sheriff over wages and their share of health
care premiums. A union is certified on a petition signed by 51 percent of
deputies, or, if the Sheriff challenges it, by a secret ballot run by an
American Arbitration Association neutral. Contracts run one to four years, need
ratification by the unit, the Sheriff and the Commissioners, and extra funding
still needs the Commissioners' approval. Correctional deputies may not strike.
The bill creates bargaining rights, so a yes vote is `for`.
