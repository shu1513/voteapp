# Maine batch-06 — selection (final batch)

**18 rolls / 9 measures / 1,137 records.** This closes the Maine campaign:
**110 of the 433 divided-and-enacted rolls are now judged across 55 measures**,
and what remains does not survive the filters.

Eight areas. **`cost_of_living_reduction` is new to Maine**, and **LD 215 is
only the second `against` in the whole campaign** after batch-01's LD 556 — it
is a real one, not a manufactured balance: the Act cuts the general-assistance
ceiling for residents of large recovery homes to 70 percent.

## Two title traps

- **LD 2058** reads "Clarify the Requirement That Municipal and County Jails Be
  Available at All Times for Detention of Arrested Persons." The operative
  change is a **carve-out for federal civil immigration holds** — jails are no
  longer required to be available when the person is detained solely for a
  civil violation of federal immigration law.
- **LD 784**'s title suggests a workers' compensation or screening mandate. The
  Act sits in the **insurance** code and creates a rebuttable presumption
  against a **health insurance carrier** that denies coverage for the listed
  screenings.

## Dropped after a full read

- **LD 698** ("Sustain Emergency Homeless Shelters") — the title is the
  opposite of the mechanism. The Act **redirects $2,982,000 away from the
  Housing Opportunities for Maine Fund and $1,491,000 away from the Housing
  First Fund into the General Fund**, and deallocates one-time Housing First
  money. Funding shelters by defunding two other housing programs runs both
  ways.
- **LD 70** ("Fund Free Health Clinics") and **LD 143** (family planning) —
  pure appropriations. LD 70's entire operative text is a $50,000-per-year
  table; LD 143's is a one-time $3,000,000 transfer of surplus. The standing
  rule that a spending vote carries no direction of its own was applied to
  LD 70 in batch-03 and is applied uniformly here rather than being narrowed
  for one measure.
- **LD 2148** (state employee premium cap) — raises the cap from CPI plus 3% to
  CPI plus 10%. Whether that falls on employees or on the State depends on
  cost-sharing the Act does not set, so no honest direction.
- **LD 1277** (removes anabolic steroids from prescription monitoring),
  **LD 1543** (Green Schools Network, advisory and funding-contingent) — too
  thin to carry a stance.

## What is left, and why the campaign stops here

**323 rolls on 160 unjudged measures remain, and none of them clears the
filters.** The residue is appropriations and study resolves, procedural
measures (legislative reimbursement, adjournment voting rules, procurement
limits, board membership), technical utility and tax bills, and
contested-direction measures already dispositioned with reasons in earlier
batches: LD 894, LD 1187, LD 713, LD 2063, LD 497, LD 2187, LD 2240, LD 702,
LD 1343, and the criminal-justice group (LD 1113, LD 1189, LD 246, LD 1710)
where the fifth filter finds no defensible direction.

Maine is finished at **6,949 records across 131 candidates and 15 research
areas**.
