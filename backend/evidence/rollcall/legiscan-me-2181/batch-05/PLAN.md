# Maine batch-05 — selection

**20 rolls / 10 measures / 1,267 records**, drawn from the 341 divided rolls on
measures that became law that batches 01-04 left. Every measure appears once
per chamber. Descriptions written in plain English from the start.

Same five filters, the same Maine stage rule (prefer the `Enactment` roll; a
recede-and-concur or post-amendment engrossment where the other chamber
amended afterwards; never a roll that failed), and the two judge gates.

Seven areas, no new ones — the pool is worked out enough that batch-05 deepens
coverage rather than widening it. **LD 785 is the batch's headline**: its title
promises tax changes, and Part A gives the **Mi'kmaq Nation a seat in the Maine
House of Representatives**, which the other three Wabanaki nations already
hold.

## Dropped after a full read

- **LD 2187** (water quality standards) — mixes clearly protective
  reclassifications, several waters moved up to Class AA, with a **loosening**
  of the "aquatic life as naturally occurs" standard for state-approved fish
  stocking and with new numeric bacteria ceilings whose direction cannot be
  settled without diffing against the prior rules. No single honest direction.
- **LD 2240** (legislative review of the Significant Wildlife Habitat rule) —
  the resolve's entire operative text authorises final adoption of a rule; the
  substance is in the rule, which is not in the measure. A description could
  not say what was approved. A no vote also plausibly turns on the rule being
  filed late rather than on habitat protection.
- **LD 497** (significant vernal pools) — codifies protective habitat
  definitions and buffers **and** codifies landowner exemptions in the same
  Act. Runs both ways.
- **LD 613** was resolved separately in batch-04.
- Appropriations (LD 609, LD 210, LD 2212), study resolves, and the measures
  batches 02-03 already dropped for stated reasons stay out.

**341 divided-and-enacted rolls on 169 unjudged measures remain.** What is left
is genuinely thin: mostly appropriations, study resolves, technical utility and
procurement bills, and contested-direction measures the fifth filter excludes.
A batch-06 would be small and would need direction calls rather than reading.
