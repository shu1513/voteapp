# Maine batch-03 — selection

**24 rolls / 12 measures / 1,512 records**, drawn from the 387 divided rolls on
measures that became law that batches 01-02 left; 363 remain after this one.
Every measure appears once per chamber.

Same five filters as before (divided → became law → nameable subject → one roll
per measure per chamber → a defensible for/against direction), the same Maine
stage rule (prefer the `Enactment` roll; a recede-and-concur when the other
chamber amended afterwards; never a roll that failed), and the two judge gates
(explicit `nay` on every label; `acknowledge_later_rolls` where a same-day pair
cannot be ordered by date).

Nine areas. Two are new to Maine: **`reduce_wealth_gap`** and
**`social_programs_and_welfare`**. LD 2051 carries the batch's only second
label — its single provision is at once a safety-net expansion and an
immigrant-inclusion measure, so it is scored per strand rather than flattened.

**Every label is `for` again.** Batch-02 explained why and it still holds: under
a Democratic trifecta the divided-and-enacted set is overwhelmingly the
majority's agenda passing over minority objection. I looked for a counterweight
and did not force one — see LD 2063 below.

## Dropped after a full read

- **LD 2063** (activities allowed under the Natural Resources Protection Act) —
  the closest thing to a counter-direction measure in the remaining pool, and
  it does not survive filter 5. The enacted text is narrow permit housekeeping
  that runs both ways: it exempts emergency flood work (but requires the stream
  be restored afterwards), exempts hand planting of native vegetation under
  tight conditions, and **narrows** an existing exemption so that seawalls,
  jetties and breakwaters no longer count as a "pier, wharf or dock". No honest
  single direction.
- **LD 613** (Death with Dignity Act access) — the session's most-voted measure
  in the remaining pool, 7 divided rolls. Held on the Illinois SB 1950
  precedent: no research area carries an honest direction on medical aid in
  dying. Expect to be asked.
- **LD 2157** (drones over prisons) — real but narrow, and `public_safety` is
  already carried by LD 670. Its penalty structure also changed twice between
  the majority report, the minority report, and a Senate amendment.
- Appropriations (LD 609, LD 210, LD 2212) and study resolves (LD 1494, LD 109,
  LD 1749) stay out on the standing rules.
- **363 divided-and-enacted rolls on 180 unjudged measures remain**; the ledger
  is `../survey/divided-enacted-worklist.tsv`.
