## HB 130
House Substitute 1 adds 14 Del. C. § 4020 and 19 Del. C. § 1320. A public
employer must give the exclusive representative each new unit employee's contact
details within 14 days of hire and a full list every January and October from
2026. The union may email members at work addresses under the employer's
technology policy, and may meet them in public buildings if it does not disrupt
operations, not for partisan campaigning, with any extra costs chargeable. The
bill widens union access, so a yes vote is `for`.

## SB 24
New 14 Del. C. § 4011A and 19 Del. C. § 1311B: when a union shows signed
authorizations from over 50 percent of an appropriate unit, the Public
Employment Relations Board must certify it without an election. The rest is
drafting-manual cleanup (30 percent written as 30%). Certification by majority
sign-up widens the right to organize, so a yes vote is `for`.
