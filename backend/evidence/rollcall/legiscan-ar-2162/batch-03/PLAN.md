## The one call worth explaining

**SB 307, the Generating Arkansas Jobs Act, is judged as corporate accountability, not cost of
living.** As an affordability question it runs two ways: it should speed new generation, and it
makes customers pay a return on plants that are not yet serving anyone. As a corporate
accountability question it runs one way — every provision loosens a constraint on the utility.
It lets companies collect a profit during construction, repeals the eight percent cap on how fast
wholesale electric cooperatives may raise rates, puts the Public Service Commission on clocks
whose expiry makes a company's filing effective by itself, narrows who may take part in hearings,
and drops the rule that put the papers in local libraries. That is the test Montana's HB 740
settled, applied here in the opposite direction.
