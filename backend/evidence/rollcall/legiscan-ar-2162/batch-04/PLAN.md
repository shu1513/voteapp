holds no Arkansas roll-call records. This batch closes the 2025 Regular Session: every gated
measure is accounted for in `../survey/DISPOSITIONS.md`, and nothing is left open.

## Without a stance

Divided, enacted and of clear public salience, but no area carries an honest direction. Imported
rather than dropped, following Ohio H.B. 116 and Alabama HB 2.

- **HB 1489** adds nitrogen gas as a method of execution. Both "carrying out lawful sentences" and
  "reducing state killing" are defensible readings of public safety, so the direction is contested.
- **SB 640** ends every sitting State Library Board member's term and lets the Governor appoint the
  whole board. Concentrating appointment power is not by itself an abuse of office, which is what
  `anti_corruption` covers.
- **HB 1929** bars state agencies from using the term "West Bank". `peaceful_foreign_policy` is
  about diplomacy over war, which a naming rule does not reach.
- **SB 437** creates a permit system for large wind farms. Its wildlife, water, noise and
  decommissioning rules genuinely protect community and environmental health, while its setback
  numbers are strict enough to make new wind development difficult. The act points both ways
  inside `environment_and_public_health` itself.
- **HB 1945** widens public school choice. School choice financing takes no stance across this
  campaign, following Texas SB 2, Georgia SB 82 and the Alabama line.

⚠ **`general` is not offered by any state legislative office**, so these tags do not rank for
users. The record's text is what carries the value. That is a known limitation of the no-stance
route across the whole campaign, recorded here rather than left implicit.
