# Arkansas 2025 Regular Session — every gated measure, and what happened to it

The campaign's gate is a divided vote (the losing side at least a quarter of the winning
side) on a measure that became law. That gate caught **131 roll calls on 95 measures**. This
file accounts for every one of them, so nothing is left silently unread.


## Summary

| Outcome | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| Judged and imported, with a stance | 29 |
| Judged and imported, no stance honestly available | 9 |
| Dropped after the act was read in full | 28 |
| **Total gated measures** | **77** |

The count above covers the 66 measures worked in this run plus the 11 already imported in
batch-01 and the one batch-01 dropped. The drop rate across the whole session is about 42 percent,
which is close to California's 47 percent. That rate is expected, not a shortfall: the strongest
measures are taken first, and the tail is thin.

## Imported without a stance

Divided, enacted and of clear public salience, but no research area carries an honest
direction. Imported rather than dropped, following Ohio H.B. 116 and Alabama HB 2. **Note that
`general` is not offered by any state legislative office, so the tag does not rank for users; the
record's text is the value.**

| Measure | Batch | Why no stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HB 1221 | batch-02 | voids petition signatures gathered after the next general election and expires the Attorney General's certification. It narrows how long citizens have to qualify a measure without touching whether a signature is genuine, so neither election_integrity nor civil_rights describes it honestly |
| HB 1222 | batch-02 | lets the Attorney General reject a proposed measure whose text conflicts with federal law, and bars a sponsor from filing more than one wording of the same idea. Content gatekeeping, not verification |
| HB 1489 | batch-04 | adds nitrogen gas as a second method of execution, shields nitrogen suppliers from disclosure, and keeps death sentences in place when a method is struck down. Both 'carrying out lawful sentences' and 'reducing state killing' are defensible readings of public safety, so the direction is contested |
| HB 1574 | batch-02 | limits paid petition canvassing to Arkansas residents, and for statewide measures to people domiciled here, with a $2,500 fine per canvasser on sponsors who knowingly hire outside those limits. It restricts who may take part rather than testing the honesty of the transaction |
| HB 1929 | batch-04 | bars state agencies from using the term 'West Bank', requiring 'Judea and Samaria' instead. peaceful_foreign_policy is about diplomacy over war, which a naming rule does not reach, and no other area fits a symbolic statement |
| HB 1945 | batch-04 | expands public school choice, including transfers inside a district. School choice financing takes no stance across this campaign (the Texas SB 2, Georgia SB 82 and Alabama line) |
| SB 437 | batch-04 | creates a permitting regime for utility-scale wind projects. Its wildlife, groundwater, noise and setback rules genuinely protect community and environmental health, while its siting numbers are strict enough to make new wind development difficult, so the act points both ways inside environment_and_public_health itself |
| SB 640 | batch-04 | ends every sitting State Library Board member's term at once and lets the Governor appoint the whole board. Concentrating appointment power is not by itself an abuse of office, so anti_corruption does not fit, and no other area does |
| SJR 15 | batch-02 | would create economic development districts, put their borrowing outside constitutional debt limits, exempt property inside them from ordinary taxation, and repeal anything in the Constitution that conflicts. The strands are inseparable in one ballot question a voter can only accept or reject whole, which is the Missouri HJR 3 shape |

## Superseded rolls

28 of the 131 gated rolls are not the last kept floor vote their chamber took on the measure.
A later vote in the same chamber was cast on the text that became law, so the earlier divided roll
is not the member's final position on it. Those are marked `superseded` in the worklist and were
not judged. This is the shape that produced a retraction in the federal campaign, so it is applied
as a rule rather than case by case.
