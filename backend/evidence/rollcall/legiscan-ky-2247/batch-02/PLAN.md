## What was dropped

Nineteen measures were read and dropped, with a reason on every roll in the
worklist. Five kinds:

- **Budget, appropriations and revenue measures** carry no single subject:
  HB 500, HB 501, HB 503, HB 504, HB 757, HB 869.
- **HB 2 on Medicaid was dropped for a different reason.** The Governor vetoed it
  in part, so the two override rolls that survive filter 4 are votes on the
  vetoed items, not on the Act. Kentucky publishes the veto message only as a
  scan with no text layer, so which items those were could not be established.
  The House's own passage roll is on a text the free conference committee later
  replaced. This is the same reason batch-01 left HB 2 alone.
- **Omnibus Acts with no single direction**: HB 257 (school accountability),
  HB 379 (four unrelated postsecondary subjects), HB 607 (consolidated local
  government), HB 619 (the KCTCS board), HB 904 (three unrelated gaming
  subjects), HB 314 (the Communications Network Authority board).
- **A measure that relaxes and conditions in the same Act**: SB 263, the school
  of innovation waiver process. The 2025 session dropped SB 207 on exactly this
  ground.
- **Too thin to name a subject**: HB 96 (a working group's membership), HB 142
  (wildlife destruction tags), HB 652 (moving the school mapping program to the
  911 board), SB 39 (hunting and fishing license conditions), SB 278 (off-duty
  work by state troopers).
