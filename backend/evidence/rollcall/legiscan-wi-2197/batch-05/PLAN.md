# Wisconsin batch-05 — plan

Batch-05 continues the **vetoed pool** under the scope rules declared in
batch-02's PLAN.md: filter 2 is read as "passed both chambers", all prose is
conditional, every description names how the measure died, and the text read is
the enrolled print.

Local database only. Production holds no Wisconsin roll-call records.

## What is in batch-05

The **health, gender and civil rights strand**. Eleven measures were read, seven
selected, four dropped.

Selected: AB 103, AB 104, SB 405, SB 431, SB 799, SB 4, SB 214.

That is **10 roll calls**. One slot was dropped on the version rule.

## ⚠ An open operator question governs two of the drops

Wyoming's batch-01 dropped SF0044, a college sports bill that designates teams by
biological sex, and raised it with the operator as **a decision to be made once
for every state, not state by state**. It has not been decided.

Wisconsin AB 100 (school sports and locker rooms) and AB 102 (university and
technical college sports and locker rooms) are direct analogs. They are dropped
here on the same reasoning so the campaign stays consistent, and they are ready
to be added the moment the operator rules the other way. See JUDGING.md for why
the other three gender-related measures do not fall under the same question.

**Update:** the operator ruled in Wyoming batch-04 (`civil_rights`, yes =
against). AB 100 and AB 102 are imported in batch-08.
