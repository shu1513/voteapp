## What is in batch-04

The **immigration and foreign-adversary strand**. Ten measures were read, nine
selected, one dropped.

Immigration: AB 24, AB 281, AB 308.
Foreign adversary: AB 415, AB 662, AB 663, AB 673, SB 7, SB 10.

That is **14 roll calls**. One slot was dropped on the version rule; see
JUDGING.md.

## Why these two subjects sit in one batch

They are not the same policy, and they get different labels. They are read
together because Wisconsin's 2025-26 majority ran them as one program of bills
in the same weeks, and reading them together is the only way to notice that six
of them share a single definition of "foreign adversary" pinned to the same
federal list. Reading them apart would have meant reading that definition six
times without seeing it was the same one.
