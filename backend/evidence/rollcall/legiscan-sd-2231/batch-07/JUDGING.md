# South Dakota batch-07: judging

## HB 1217
Read from the House Education engrossed text (`ls_text.py`). It would have
barred school districts, cooperatives, technical colleges and the Board of
Regents from using public money to support a union: no payroll dues deduction,
no pay raised to cover dues, no worker data beyond public records law without
the worker's written consent, no required meetings with a union, no union
mailings, and no paid time for union activities except two carve-outs in
section 3: a worker's own earned or donated leave, and on-duty representational
work (grievances, bargaining) that the union repays at pro rata cost. The
attorney general could seek civil penalties, and anyone could sue for an
injunction and damages. Every change narrows union rights, so a yes vote is
`against`.
