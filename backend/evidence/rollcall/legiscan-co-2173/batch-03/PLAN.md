**SB 25-130 carries two labels.** Its core is a state version of the federal
duty to treat anyone who arrives needing emergency care, which is access to
care. But the act also defines emergency services to include an abortion or a
sterilisation when that is what stabilises the patient, and that is an operative
duty on the facility, not a gloss. Both strands are labelled, following the
per-strand pattern set in Florida SB 700.

**SB 25-083 is scored corporate accountability, not health care**, following the
Indiana SB 475 precedent for physician non-compete bans: the act binds employers
and what they may put in a contract.
