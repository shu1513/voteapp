# Utah 2026 batch-08 — how each measure was judged

Sources: the Office of Legislative Research and General Counsel's summary as an
index, and the enrolled text, read through `ut_text.py`, as ground truth.

## HB 192 Vehicle Inspection Fee Amendments

Deletes the statutory caps on a safety inspection station's labor fee: $14 for
motorcycles and street-legal all-terrain vehicles, $30 for most motor vehicles,
and $40 for four-wheel drive and vehicles that need a hub or axle removed. A
station may now charge "a reasonable fee for labor", and must post it before
the inspection.

The area is defined as lowering household costs. Supporters argue the caps were
stale and pushed stations out of the program. But the act's mechanism is to
remove a price limit on a required service, so the record files it as
`cost_of_living_reduction` / against. The description does not claim that every
car needs an inspection.

## SB 314 Sleep Disorders Education Amendments

Directs the Division of Population Health to develop public education on sleep
health and named sleep disorders, including narcolepsy, idiopathic hypersomnia,
sleep apnea and circadian rhythm disorders. The aims are awareness, earlier
diagnosis and treatment, less stigma, provider education, and the safety risks
of untreated disorders, including driving. The division may partner with
insurers, provider groups, school districts and others.
`environment_and_public_health` / for. The no side's objection is new state
spending, a different axis, so it gets no tag.
