# Utah 2026 batch-09 — how each measure was judged

Sources: the Office of Legislative Research and General Counsel's summary as an
index, and the enrolled text, read through `ut_text.py`, as ground truth.

## HB 48 Criminal and Juvenile Justice Changes

A minor tried as an adult for aggravated murder is normally housed in a youth
secure care facility until age 25. The act lets the district court commit the
minor to the Department of Corrections instead if the minor was 17 at the time,
is 18 or older at sentencing, and would be a security risk to others in secure
care. A prosecutor may also move the Board of Pardons and Parole to order the
same transfer. The act also adds an "alternative recidivism metric" that state
reports must include. `public_safety_and_crime_control` / for.

## HB 54 Boating Safety Education

Creates a yearly watercraft operator safety course (motorboats and sailboats)
that should take under 20 minutes on average. From January 1, 2027, an operator
must carry proof of completion. A livery must see proof or give a safety
briefing before renting. A violation is an infraction. Injury prevention on the
water is the safety area: `public_safety_and_crime_control` / for.

## HB 185 Carbon Credit Amendments

Creates the Carbon Credit Litigation Fund. The attorney general may spend it to
prevent a federal requirement that the state adopt or join a cap-and-trade
program, mandatory carbon emissions reporting, a climate remediation program,
or a greenhouse gas claim against the state. It may also be spent to recover
carbon credits sold out of state by fraud. Money comes from carbon credit
judgments and settlements, plus appropriations. State entities must report
each carbon credit sale to the state auditor. The fund exists to oppose
emissions rules, so `environment_and_public_health` / against.

## HB 188 Juvenile Justice Amendments

A school official must refer a minor directly to court for an alleged drug
possession offense on school property when the minor has two earlier alleged
offenses there. Before, officials could choose court or alternative
interventions. The principal must now also notify the student's parent. A
juvenile probation officer may not offer a nonjudicial adjustment for a
misdemeanor distribution or possession-with-intent charge against a minor 12 or
older. `public_safety_and_crime_control` / for.

## HB 273 Classroom Technology Amendments

Schools must minimize non-essential screen time. Classroom technology must have
demonstrated educational value. Each school system must adopt a balanced
technology policy and an AI use policy based on state board models, with the
models as the default. The AI model bars teachers from using generative AI to
grade student work or make high-stakes decisions on their own. It also bars AI
tools used for biometric surveillance or psychological profiling, and requires
written notice to parents. The clearest direction is the AI rulebook:
`ai_regulation` / for. The screen-time strand has no agreed direction on
learning, so it gets no tag.

## HB 331 Criminal Offenses Modifications

Renames "threat of terrorism" to "terrorism" and covers completed acts. Creates
three new crimes:
- committing a crime in coordination with a federally designated foreign
  terrorist organization, or acting unlawfully as a foreign government's agent
  (third degree felony);
- wearing a mask to hide from law enforcement while committing a crime in
  public, or while congregating with people one knows are committing one (class
  A misdemeanor);
- acting with two or more others to keep someone from entering or leaving a
  house of worship (third degree felony, with a civil suit).

The mask offense raises a protest-rights concern, and the house-of-worship
offense protects a civil right. So `civil_rights` runs both ways and is not
tagged. `public_safety_and_crime_control` / for.

## HB 419 Environmental Legal Action Amendments

Utah's bond rule for environmental actions had excluded Division of Oil, Gas,
and Mining coal permits. The act deletes that exclusion. A plaintiff challenging
such a permit must now post a bond before getting a preliminary injunction or
administrative stay, and is liable for the defendant's damages if the plaintiff
does not ultimately prevail. It raises the cost of challenging a coal mining
permit, so `environment_and_public_health` / against.

## HB 436 Moderate Income Housing Infrastructure Amendments

A municipality that must file a moderate income housing report now reports new
certificates of occupancy. If its housing units grew 2.5% or more in the past 12
months, the Transportation Commission must give priority consideration to a
significant regional transportation project that benefits it, inside or outside
its borders. All priority consideration together may raise a project's score by
no more than 7%. The act rewards housing growth: `housing_affordability` / for.

## HB 539 Public Safety Modifications

Rewrites the domestic violence offense list. It adds human trafficking and
felony or class A abuse or exploitation of a vulnerable adult. Bail gains a
rebuttable presumption of danger for a felony or class A domestic violence
offense committed while on probation, parole or pretrial release for a domestic
violence offense. Police must submit charges within five business days unless
they have reasonable cause for delay. Workplace violence protective orders now
cover nonprofit volunteers.

It also cuts the other way in two places. A jail release agreement may not bar
a parent arrested for domestic violence in a child's presence from contacting
their own child, when the child and the child's other parent are not the
victims. The initial appearance moves from "within one judicial day" to
"without unnecessary delay" under Rule 9. The main thrust is tougher
enforcement: `public_safety_and_crime_control` / for. The description does not
claim the act covers adult siblings, because siblings were already cohabitants.

## HB 540 Judicial Transparency and Information Access Amendments

From January 1, 2027, the Judicial Council must run one website to search all
public court records. It needs registration and is free up to 50 searches and
50 downloads a month. Every public court proceeding outside justice court must
be audio recorded, and recordings are provided on request. The Judicial Council
must also require yearly financial disclosures from judges and court
commissioners and post them publicly. `anti_corruption` / for.

## SB 58 Public School Attendance Amendments

School systems must review attendance data for students at risk of chronic
absenteeism (10% of days or more). They must use tiered interventions and tell
parents within two instructional days of an unexcused absence. The state board
must set attendance data standards and audit them. A system that fails must
file a corrective plan and may have state money withheld, unless its only
failure is tracking excused absences. `public_education_quality` / for.

## Dropped or deferred

- **SB 26**, mixed. Deleting "not liable unless negligent" tightens pesticide
  liability. The new ornamental and turf technician permit (a yearly video
  course and indirect supervision) and the repeal of the business qualifying
  party both loosen who may apply herbicide.
- **SB 251**, not recognizable. It covers the reimbursement procedure for local
  enforcement costs and renames one administrative penalty as a civil penalty.
- **SB 153**, operator direction call. From May 25, 2026, every withheld or
  private voter record becomes public unless the voter proves at-risk status.
  At-risk covers domestic violence, police, military, public figures and
  protective orders. It also limits use of the voter list to political and
  other lawful purposes, and makes misuse a class A misdemeanor. The Senate's
  19-5 concurrence was reconsidered, and the 22-7 revote six minutes later is
  the decisive roll.
