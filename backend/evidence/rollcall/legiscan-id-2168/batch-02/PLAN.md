## Version check

Three measures carry an engrossed print. In each case filter 4 lands both
chambers on the text that became law:

- **House Bill 109**, chapter 340. Senate passed the amended text 25-10 on
  March 26, House passed the engrossed text 48-20 on March 31.
- **House Bill 180**, chapter 325. Senate passed the amended text 24-9 on
  April 1, House passed the engrossed text 48-20 on April 3.
- **Senate Bill 1198**, chapter 317. House passed the amended text 52-17 on
  April 3, Senate passed it 23-12 on April 4.

House Bill 79, House Bill 187 and Senate Bill 1069 have no engrossed print, so
both chambers voted the introduced text and that text became law.
