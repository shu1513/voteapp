## Version check

Six of the twelve were amended, and in every case the kept roll sits on the
amended text that became law. The engrossed print was read, not the introduced
one.

| measure | what happened |
| --- | --- |
| House Bill 96 | Senate amended it, then passed the amended text 20-11. The House concurred. Read from `H0096E1`. |
| House Bill 339 | Senate amended it, then passed the amended text 27-7. Read from `H0339E1`. |
| Senate Bill 1007 | Senate amended it in committee of the whole, then passed the amended text 25-9. House passed it unchanged. Read from `S1007E1`. |
| Senate Bill 1046 | Senate amended it, then passed the amended text 27-8 the next day. House passed it unchanged. Read from `S1046E1`. |
| Senate Bill 1141 | Senate amended it, then passed the amended text 27-8. House passed it unchanged. Read from `S1141E1`. |
| Senate Bill 1210 | Senate amended it and passed 22-13. The House amended it again and passed the second engrossed text 44-23. The Senate then concurred 27-6, which was not divided. The kept roll is the House one, on the text that became law. Read from `S1210E2`. |

Senate Bill 1210 is the case the version check exists for. Reading the
introduced print would have described a Covid-only mandate ban rather than the
general one that was enacted.

The other six have no engrossed print, so the introduced text became law.
