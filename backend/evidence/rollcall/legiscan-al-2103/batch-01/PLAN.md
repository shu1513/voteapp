# Alabama 2024 batch-01 — selection plan

## How the batch was selected

The five filters, with the roll-attribution check run first.

0. **The roll must be filed under the right bill.** Every divided roll's printed roll call number was
   checked against its own bill's history, the test the 2026 session made necessary. **All 29 pass.**
   System A rolls carry no roll call number, and every System B number is in its own bill's history.
1. **Divided** — 29 of 824 kept floor votes. This number depends entirely on reading the session's
   two caption systems correctly; see `../CODE-FINDINGS.md`.
2. **Consequential** — the measure became law. 10 rolls on 6 measures.
3. **A nameable subject.** Nothing dropped: none of the enacted divided measures is a local act.
4. **One roll per measure per chamber, preferring the vote on the enacted text.** SB 231's House vote
   on the conference report replaces its earlier House passage vote.
5. **A defensible direction, or a deliberate no-stance import.** Nothing dropped; three measures are
   imported with no stance.

## Nothing was dropped at filter 5

Every divided-and-enacted measure in this session is in the batch. Three carry no stance rather than
being dropped, on the campaign's rule: import without a stance when the vote is divided, enacted and
of clear public salience but no research area carries an honest direction.
