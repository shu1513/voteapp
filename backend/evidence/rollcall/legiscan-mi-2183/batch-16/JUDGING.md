## HB 5520 — grade 3 reading retention

Read from the engrossed print of section 1280f with the fixed markup reader. Of
60,070 characters, 7,635 are marked as added and 69 as deleted; the deletions are
renumbering and one "and". The unmarked text is the section as amended by 2024
PA 146 (screening, the multi-tiered system of support, and science of reading
instruction), which the bill reprints because it amends the section.

The added text:

- Subsection (12) notices gain "and determine whether a pupil may be advanced to
  grade 4" and a new item telling parents the pupil is subject to being retained
  in grade 3, subject to subsections (33) and (34).
- **(32)** Beginning with pupils in grade 3 in 2027-2028, a pupil whose parent
  received that notice may not be enrolled in grade 4 until the pupil is
  proficient on the state English language arts assessment, on an alternative
  standardized reading assessment approved by the superintendent of public
  instruction, or through a portfolio of work samples. The school must send a
  second notice explaining the retention, the alternatives, the right to ask for
  a good cause exemption within 30 days, and the right to a meeting.
- **(33)** The retention does not apply if the pupil is proficient in all other
  subject areas, including science and social studies shown by a portfolio and
  the grade 3 teacher's determination.
- **(34)** Good cause exemptions only for: a pupil with an individualized
  education program or section 504 plan whose team decides so; a limited English
  proficient pupil with under 3 years in an English learner program; a pupil
  enrolled under 2 years whose previous school did not provide an appropriate
  reading improvement plan; a pupil with 2 or more years of intensive
  intervention who was previously retained; and a timely parent request the
  superintendent finds in the pupil's best interests.
- **(35)** Procedure: the grade 3 teacher recommends with documentation, the
  superintendent or charter chief administrator decides in writing (final), and
  parents are notified at least 30 days before school starts.
- **(36)** Yearly reporting to CEPI of the number retained and the number
  promoted by each exemption.

The description names the start year, the three ways to show proficiency, the
other-subjects exception, and the five exemption classes, because a retention
rule without its exits would overstate what the bill does.
