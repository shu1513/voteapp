## Why this one had its own batch

HB 5520 was deferred in batch-04 because its engrossed print is a 60,000
character reprint of section 1280f of the Revised School Code and it has no
Fiscal Agency analysis. Read with the fixed markup reader, the print carries
only about 7,600 characters of added text and 69 of deleted text (mostly
renumbering). The large unmarked middle is the existing 2024 dyslexia screening
and intervention law (2024 PA 146).

The additions are one policy: new subsections (32) to (36) bring back grade 3
retention, which Michigan repealed in 2023, starting with pupils in grade 3 in
2027-2028. See JUDGING.md for the exceptions and exemptions.

## Label

`public_education_quality` is defined as strengthening student outcomes through
effective teaching, standards, funding and accountability. A promotion standard
tied to reading proficiency is an accountability measure, and batch-04 kept the
same package's science-of-reading bills (HB 4159, HB 5819, HB 5820) as for.
Whether retention helps children is argued both ways; the description sets out
exactly who would be held back and who would be exempt so a reader can judge.
