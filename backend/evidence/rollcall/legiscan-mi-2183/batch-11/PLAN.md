## Label calls

- **HB 4467 and HB 4466 go to `civil_rights`**, matching batch-03's HB 4024 and
  HB 4469, where Michigan bills drawing lines by birth-certificate sex went to
  civil_rights against. Both bills are kept: HB 4467 is the ban and HB 4466 the
  licensing sanction, two separate acts (batch-01's SB 293 and SB 294 precedent).
- **HB 4915 goes to `civil_rights` against**, the Ohio SB 1 and Texas SB 12
  precedent that ending diversity, equity or bias programs reads against the
  area.
- **HB 5974 goes to `data_privacy` against.** It removes a minor's exclusive
  control over records of care the minor lawfully obtained alone, and bars
  providers from limiting a parent's access except under federal law.
- **HB 4356 goes to `election_integrity` for**, following the South Dakota rule
  that voter roll maintenance is about the accuracy of the rolls, not who may
  vote. Wyoming left the same question to the operator; this batch follows the
  South Dakota call and says so here. The engrossed print says 8 years, not the
  10 in the introduced-bill summary.
- **HB 4602 goes to `election_integrity` for.** It also strikes 2002 provisions
  about the move to a uniform state voting system, which the House analysis
  calls obsolete; the description leaves them out.

## Dropped from this block

- **HB 4468**, insurance coverage of care after a gender transition. Written as
  part of the minors' ban package; labeling it healthcare_affordability for would
  misstate what the vote was about.
- **HB 5299**, a technical companion to the no-fault bill in batch-09.
