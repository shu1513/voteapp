## Label calls

- **SB 111, SB 257 and SB 260** create new protections or remedies for victims
  (a new order, longer time to sue, liability for institutions that failed to
  act). `public_safety_and_crime_control` names accountability, and these widen
  it without raising any criminal penalty.
- **SB 78** follows the South Dakota SB 83 precedent: a justice-system reform
  that lets a court clear an old juvenile-age conviction, under strict
  conditions, reads as for the area's "justice system performance" strand.
- **SB 154** goes to `womens_reproductive_rights`: its subject is access to
  reproductive health care, and the criminal penalties serve that end.
