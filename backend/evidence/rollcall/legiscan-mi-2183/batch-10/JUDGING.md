## The measures

### HB 4338, HB 4339, HB 4342 — sanctuary policies
HB 4338 (the Local Government Sanctuary Policy Prohibition Act) and HB 4339 (the
County Law Enforcement Protection Act) are parallel new acts, one for cities,
villages and townships and one for counties. Each voids any rule that limits a
peace officer or local official from communicating or cooperating with federal
officials about immigration status, gives existing rules 60 days to comply,
lets a resident sue or complain to the attorney general, requires the attorney
general to sue, and orders an injunction, amendment or repeal, damages, costs
and fees when a court finds a violation. Both passed on the same floor
substitute adopted the day before. HB 4342 adds section 21(4) to the revenue
sharing act: the state treasurer withholds all payments from a unit that
enforces a rule violating either act, for each fiscal year beginning on and
after October 1, 2025.

### HB 4340 and HB 4341 — citizenship for state programs
Two one-section bills with the same sentence: no services, grants or program
participation under the act unless the individual is a U.S. citizen or a
qualified alien under 8 USC 1641, except as otherwise provided under federal
law. HB 4340 amends the Social Welfare Act; HB 4341 the State Housing
Development Authority Act.

### HB 4515, HB 5145, HB 5812, HB 6013 — public assistance
HB 4515: photo and signature on the Bridge card, for every authorized adult user.
HB 5145: no self-attestation of identity, income or residency at application or
annual redetermination; the document lists in the description are the bill's
own; before denying, the department must use collateral contacts or data
matches. HB 5812: no federal work requirement waiver under 7 USC 2015(o)(4)
without specific legislative authorization, and no use of the discretionary
exemptions under 2015(o)(6)(F), with the federal good-cause rule for temporary
absence preserved. HB 6013: a Bridge card used out of state for more than 90
consecutive days is suspended, subject to federal law, until residency or
eligibility is verified.
