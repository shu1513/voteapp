## Direction

`immigration` is defined as welcoming immigration through a lawful, orderly and
humane system. A mandate that local police cooperate with federal immigration
enforcement, and a bar on state help for immigrants who are not "qualified
aliens" under federal law, read as against that area. This follows the Texas
SB 8 and Ohio SB 172 precedent.

`social_programs_and_welfare` is defined as supporting vulnerable people through
effective safety-net programs. Each of these four bills adds a condition or a
hurdle to getting or keeping benefits: a photo on the card, documents instead of
self-attestation, the loss of the state's work-requirement waivers and
exemptions, and suspension after out-of-state use. Supporters describe each as
program integrity; the description says what the bill does, and the label
follows the Texas SB 379 precedent that restricting benefits is against the
area. HB 5145 carries its own safeguard (the state must try data matches before
denying help), and the description says so.
