## Why the permit bills are government_efficiency and not environment

`government_efficiency` is defined as improving service delivery and reducing
waste. Each permit bill sets a deadline, a refund for a missed deadline, a duty
to cite the rule behind a decision, or a limit on late changes to an
application. None changes what a permit requires or who needs one. Bills that
do change the substance of wetland protection (HB 5536 and HB 5502) are in
batch-07 under `environment_and_public_health`. HB 6038 and HB 5938 sit closest
to that line; their descriptions state exactly what the environment department
could no longer do so a reader can judge the effect.

The rule-making process package (HB 4039, HB 5815 to HB 5818) was dropped rather
than put here, because each of those bills changes how rules are made or
reviewed, and whether that improves government is the dispute itself.
