LegiScan session 2183, the 103rd Legislature, 2025-2026. Not-enacted scope.

**9 roll calls, 9 measures.** All nine are Senate votes. A Michigan Senate roll
reaches far fewer candidates than a House roll (about 16 against 93), because
only 17 of the 38 senators are mapped to a November 2026 candidate.

## Label calls

- **SB 757 and SB 758 go to `data_privacy` for**, the Colorado SB 51 and South
  Dakota precedent for app and platform rules protecting minors' data. **SB 760
  goes to `ai_regulation` for**; it governs what an AI chatbot may do with a
  minor.
- **SB 1041, SB 1042 and SB 1043 are kept as three rolls**, not collapsed. Each
  creates a separate act for a different market (lodging, goods and services,
  energy), and each senator cast three separate votes.

## Dropped from this block

- **SB 759**, which adds Kids Code violations to the consumer protection act.
  An enforcement companion to SB 758.
- **SB 361 to SB 364**, cross-reference updates for SB 360.
- **SB 529, SB 530, SB 531**, petition circulation rules (ballot-access
  mechanics, the Arkansas, North Dakota and South Dakota rule).
