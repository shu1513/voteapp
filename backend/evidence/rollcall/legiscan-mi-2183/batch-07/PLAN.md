LegiScan session 2183, the 103rd Legislature, 2025-2026. Not-enacted scope.

**5 roll calls, 5 measures.** All five are House votes.

No label carries a direction for a no vote.

**HB 4445 follows batch-01's HB 4350.** That enacted deer feeding bill was
labeled environment_and_public_health, yea against, because the state's
baiting and feeding limits exist to slow chronic wasting disease. HB 4445 goes
further and allows baiting statewide during the deer seasons.

**HB 5069's list comes from the engrossed print, not the analysis.** The House
Fiscal Agency summary of the introduced bill lists potassium bromate; the floor
amendment adopted on the day of the vote removed it, and the description does not
mention it.

## Dropped from this block

- **HB 5501**, wetland mitigation. It eases mitigation schedules and ratios and
  lets developers buy temporary credits, but it also adds financial assurance
  duties for high-risk and delayed mitigation. Two directions.
- **HB 4192, HB 4257, HB 4265**, a lower groundwater permit fee for small mobile
  home parks and campgrounds, and two anaerobic digester definition bills.
  Narrow or technical.
- **HB 4073, HB 4421**, warrant requirements for natural resources and
  environment officers: weaker enforcement against stronger property privacy.
- **HB 4917**, butter graders. Narrow.
