# Michigan batch-07: judging

Not-enacted measures. Each description comes from the **engrossed print**, read
with the fixed markup reader described in batch-06/JUDGING.md, and the House
Fiscal Agency analysis used as an index.

## The measures

### HB 5707, small ponds
New subdivisions in two places. Section 30103(1)(q) and (r) exempt from the
inland lakes and streams permit the digging or building of a pond on land zoned
residential or agricultural, if the pond will be under 1 acre, is for
noncommercial residential use, and is not connected to a lake, river or stream,
plus placing the spoils on adjacent upland or at the site. New 30103(3) makes
those two subdivisions retroactive to January 1, 2020. Section 30305(2)(p) and
(q) add the same pond and spoils exemption to the uses allowed in a wetland
without a permit; that half is not retroactive, and the description says only
the lakes and streams half reaches back.

### HB 4445, deer baiting
Adds definitions of deer or elk baiting and feeding, and a new 40111a(2): "An
individual may engage in deer baiting in this state during the open seasons for
deer." The House analysis notes that a Natural Resources Commission order bans
baiting and feeding in the Lower Peninsula since the chronic wasting disease
response. Labeled to match batch-01's HB 4350.

### HB 5536, the wetland definition
New 30301(1)(o): outside the local ordinance sections, a wetland must be
adjacent to a water of the United States **and** have a continuous surface
connection to it, with a natural interruption such as a seiche or dry spell not
breaking the connection. New (p) keeps the current definition for the local
ordinance sections 30307 and 30308, but strikes the "more than 5 acres" route to
qualifying. The description says the local test keeps its other routes and loses
that one.

### HB 5502, accidentally created wetlands
Adds 30305(4)(g), "any other human activity that was not specifically intended
to create wetlands", to the list of incidental wetlands not regulated, and a new
(5) putting on the department "the burden of proof in establishing that a
wetland was incidentally created in a manner other than as described under
subsection (4)". The rest of the print is renumbering and style edits, found by
diffing against HB 5707's copy of the same section.

### HB 5069, food additives
New section 7132, effective January 1, 2029: brominated vegetable oil,
propylparaben, and Red 40, Green 3, Blue 1, Blue 2 and Yellow 6. It does not
apply if the federal government bans the listed substances by that date. The
floor amendment on the day of the vote removed potassium bromate, which the
introduced-bill summary still lists.
