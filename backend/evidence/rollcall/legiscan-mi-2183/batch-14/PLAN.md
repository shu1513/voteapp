## Label calls

- **SB 462 and SB 515 go to `environment_and_public_health`** (community health
  through standards). SB 462 follows the Texas SB 2024 e-cigarette precedent.
- **SB 962 goes to `election_integrity` for.** It is the data and research
  companion to the State Voting Rights Act package (batch-03 took SB 961, SB 963
  and SB 964). A free public archive of election data speaks to an auditable and
  trusted process rather than to who may vote, so it takes election_integrity
  rather than civil_rights.
- **SB 232 and SB 807 go to `reduce_wealth_gap` for** (economic mobility and
  asset building), the South Dakota SB 63 "try another area" precedent.
- **SB 947 goes to `public_infrastructure` for**: dams are infrastructure, and the
  bill adds registration, inspection and engineering standards.

## Dropped from this block

- **SB 278**, a housing fund rewrite that opens money to middle-income
  households while removing low-income earmarks.
- **SB 968**, the insurance-tax half of the housing credit; **SB 808**, the board
  for SB 807; **SB 465 and SB 466**, tobacco companions; **SB 463**, ending
  penalties on under-21 tobacco possession inside the licensing package.
- **SB 553, SB 792, SB 793, SB 898, SB 899, SB 199, SB 213, SB 214**, tax
  increment financing, abatements and economic development machinery.
