# Michigan batch-13: judging

Not-enacted measures, all Senate votes. Each description comes from the
**engrossed print**, read with the fixed markup reader (batch-06/JUDGING.md),
and the Senate Fiscal Agency analysis used as an index.

## The measures

### SB 3 — prescription drug affordability board
New act creating the board and a stakeholder council. The board may review drugs
and set upper payment limits by rule. Section 13(6): no limit may include
dispensing fees or apply to a drug listed as currently in shortage by the FDA.
Section 13(7): no cost-per-quality-adjusted-life-year or similar measure used to
find a subpopulation for which a drug is less cost effective because of severity
of illness, age or preexisting disability. SB 4 and SB 5 (insurer and Medicaid
compliance) are dropped as companions.

### SB 29 — disparities
Section 2227 of the Public Health Code: the strategic plan adds "and inequities",
and the department webpage adds links to peer-reviewed studies and reports on
biased or unjust perinatal care, including obstetric racism and obstetric
violence.

### SB 31 — births and hospital policy
New section 21538, from January 1, 2027: a patient giving birth may have a
partner or spouse and a doula, or a doula and a companion, present throughout
the stay, subject to listed limits. Review fix: the first description said the
companion option applied "if there is no partner"; 21538(1)(a)(ii) also covers
a partner or spouse who is not available. Re-judged and the 16 records
rewritten in place (import-rerun-report.json). The policy must state the hospital's
informed consent policy and its process for accepting a midwife's transfer
information. Section 20201's hospital duties add that "sex" includes pregnancy
or lactating status.

### SB 33 — advance directives in pregnancy
Section 5507 adds that a patient advocate designation may state which
life-sustaining treatment the patient would want or not want if pregnant.
Section 5509(1)(d), which barred a patient advocate from withholding treatment
from a pregnant patient that would result in her death, is made inapplicable
from the act's effective date, and the model form's matching sentence is
replaced.

### SB 37 — gynecological and perinatal coverage
New section 3406cc of the Insurance Code, with definitions of certified nurse
midwife, health facility, midwife and physician.

### SB 296 — nurse overtime
New section 21526 from June 1, 2027. The exceptions quoted in the description
are subsection (3)(a) to (d); the 2-hour and 4-hour holds depend on unexpected
circumstances and the hospital's efforts to find relief. Subsection (2) sets 8
consecutive hours off after a shift of 12 or more hours, with up to 4 hours
waivable in 72 hours.

### SB 973 and SB 977 — exchange and reinsurance
SB 973 creates the Michigan Health Insurance Exchange Act, a 12-member board and
a nonprofit exchange that must offer qualified plans by website and hotline by
January 1, 2028. SB 977 adds section 3406nn: apply for a section 1332 innovation
waiver, establish and fund a reinsurance program if approved, and hold public
review of the draft application.
