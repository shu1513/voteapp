## Label calls

- **SB 29 goes to `civil_rights`**, matching batch-03's SB 30 from the same
  package (reports of biased perinatal care, civil_rights for).
- **SB 31 and SB 33 go to `womens_reproductive_rights` for.** The area covers
  legal access to reproductive health care and bodily autonomy; SB 31 is about
  who may be present during a birth and SB 33 lets a pregnant patient's own
  advance directive control life-sustaining treatment.
- **SB 296 goes to `labor_rights` for** (safe workplaces). It is the substantive
  bill; SB 297, its licensing and fine companion, is dropped.

## Dropped from this block

- **SB 4, SB 5**, insurer and Medicaid compliance with SB 3's limits
  (companions); **SB 974 to SB 976**, exchange companions; **SB 978**, repeal of
  an emergency-refill rule that expired in 2021.
- **SB 569**, fund transfers; **SB 707**, therapist licensing; **SB 1052**, a
  Medicaid kickback carve-out for federal model programs.
