# Michigan batch-09: judging

Not-enacted measures. Each description comes from the **engrossed print**, read
with the fixed markup reader (batch-06/JUDGING.md), and the House Fiscal Agency
analysis used as an index.

## The measures

### HB 5036, HB 5037, HB 5035 — fertility fraud
HB 5036 adds Penal Code section 219g, printed after a floor substitute adopted
the day of the vote. Subsection (1): knowingly providing false or misleading
information about the embryo or gamete, the donor's identity, or the donor's
medical history is a felony of up to 5 years or $50,000. Subsection (2): a health
professional who knowingly or recklessly uses an embryo or gamete other than the
one the patient consented to in writing, including the professional's own, faces
up to 15 years or $100,000. An anonymous donor is not a defense for a health
professional. HB 5037 adds a 15-year charging window for section 219g, open-ended
where DNA points to an unidentified person and then 15 years after
identification. HB 5035 adds Revised Judicature Act section 2980: treble damages
plus attorney fees, a separate claim per child, the four classes of plaintiff
named in the description, and a 3-year limit from discovery. HB 5035 goes to
`corporate_accountability` because it creates civil liability for providers;
the criminal bills go to `public_safety_and_crime_control`.

### HB 5574 — first responders
New section 409c. The offense needs a verbal warning, knowing and willful
violation, approaching and remaining within 25 feet, and intent to impede,
threaten or harass. 60 days or $500; injury, 2 years or $1,000; death, 15 years
or $7,500. The definition of first responder comes from HB 5575 (law enforcement
officers, corrections officers including parole and probation officers,
firefighters and emergency medical personnel), which is dropped as a companion.

### HB 5744 — "Messiah's Law"
New section 234g: intentionally discharging a firearm at a motor vehicle known or
believed to be occupied, 10 years or $10,000; 20 years or $20,000 if a minor is
known or believed to be inside. Michigan already names discharging a firearm at
a dwelling or occupied structure, but not at a vehicle, so this is a new offense
rather than a penalty increase.

### HB 5652 and HB 4949 — nuisance and noise shields
HB 5652 adds Revised Judicature Act section 2940a: an owner or occupant of land
within 5 miles of a racing facility's perimeter may not sue its owner or
operator under "any nuisance, taking, or other theory" if the track was built
first. The shield survives lawful changes to size, scope, configuration,
technology or type of racing, and does not exempt the track from environmental
or health and safety law. HB 4949 extends the existing sport shooting range
noise shield (civil liability and criminal prosecution) to a range that is
"updated or modified, including, but not limited to, ecological upgrades, safety
upgrades, or range expansion", if the change follows generally accepted
operation practices. A floor amendment was adopted the day of the vote; the
engrossed print includes it.

### HB 4582 — premises liability
New act. A possessor owes no duty to protect or warn an invitee about an open and
obvious condition, except reasonable precautions for special features that make
it effectively unavoidable or create an unreasonably high risk of severe harm.
Section 6 bars courts from adopting a Restatement (Third) reasonable-care
standard. The House analysis explains that the Michigan Supreme Court overruled
the open and obvious doctrine in 2023 (Kandil-Elsayed and Pinsky); the
description says so in one sentence.

### HB 5298 — no-fault priority
Section 3114(2) lists vehicles whose passengers get benefits from their own
policy first. The engrossed print adds (i) limousines and (j) any bus not
already listed; the transportation network company line (g) is existing law, so
the description does not claim the bill adds ride-share vehicles.

### HB 4933 — personnel agencies
Repeals Occupational Code article 10 and the license fee section. About 100
licensees; employer-paid agencies were already outside the article.
