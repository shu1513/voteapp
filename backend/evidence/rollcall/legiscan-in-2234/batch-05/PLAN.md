## The two halves

The worklist flagged HB 1302 as two halves. Both narrow protections, so the
mixed-bill rule does not apply:

- Child labor: it repeals the rule that employers of five or more workers aged
  14 to 17 register with the Department of Labor and report how many minors
  they employ, and repeals the public database of those employers and the fines
  for failing to register.
- Mine safety: it ends the separate state certificate for coal mine belt
  examiners.

The rest (election board helpers, fund wording) is technical.

## Finality

The Senate amended HB 1302 and passed it 40-7, which is not closely divided. The
House concurred 69-25, the final vote on the enacted text.
