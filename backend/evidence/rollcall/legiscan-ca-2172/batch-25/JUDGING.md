## AB 339 — Government Code section 3504.1 (Chapter 687, Statutes of 2025)
A local public agency and its designated boards give the recognized employee
organization no less than 45 days' written notice before issuing a request for
proposals or quotes, or renewing or extending a contract, for services within the
scope of work of the represented job classifications. The notice lists the
contract's duration, scope, cost, draft solicitation and reason. Subdivision (c)
allows as much notice as practicable in an emergency. Subdivision (d) exempts
public works construction-type contracts and planning, design and delivery
services for public works and building projects. The description names the
exemption in plain terms and does not claim the bill bars contracting out.

## SB 578 — Labor Code Division 7 (Chapter 771, Statutes of 2025)
"Upon appropriation of funds", the Department of Industrial Relations establishes
the program, selects qualified nonprofit organizations by competitive request,
and produces translated outreach and training materials on priority topics that
may include minimum wage, overtime, paid leave, retaliation, health and safety,
excessive heat and discrimination. The division is repealed January 1, 2031.
This is a contingent duty that binds a real party once funded, which the
California campaign treats as judgeable. The Senate slot is the concurrence roll,
the Senate's last vote on the text that became law.

## AB 393 — proposed Government Code section 19134.2 (vetoed October 3, 2025)
Before contracting out a budgeted BU-16 physician (including psychiatrist) or
BU-19 psychologist position, the prison and state hospital departments would
have prepared a cost comparison using collective bargaining rates, used an
available civil service clinician if the contractor cost more, and reported
yearly. Written in the past conditional because it did not become law.
