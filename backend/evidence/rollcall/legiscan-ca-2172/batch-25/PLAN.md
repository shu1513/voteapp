## Not reopened

- **AB 1818**, California State University bargaining: it moves one
  determination (whether the Legislature or Governor failed to fund a memorandum
  of understanding) to the Public Employment Relations Board. A neutral decider
  is procedure, not a stance for or against workers.
- **AB 2634**, a scoring preference for union-linked applicants in high road
  training partnership grants. **Held on the tally:** LegiScan's Senate roll
  1725449 reads 29-9, while the Senate history line for the same vote records
  Ayes 28, Noes 9. A mismatch between the roll and the journal has meant a member
  recorded on the wrong side every time it has been checked (the Indiana rule).
  The only other roll, the 66-7 concurrence, is not closely divided.
- **Transportation network company driver appeals**: the new court appeal runs
  equally to companies and drivers; still no stance.

## Style

Descriptions follow this campaign's California house style ("Voted to pass
Assembly Bill 339, which ..."; concurrence as "Voted to agree to the Assembly's
changes"; enacted tails "and it became law"; vetoed measures in the past
conditional with "the governor vetoed it, so it did not become law").
