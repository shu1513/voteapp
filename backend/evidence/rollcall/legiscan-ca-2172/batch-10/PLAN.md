# California batch-10 — first both-chamber batch

Drawn from `../worklist/worklist.json`, top of the ranked list. This is the first batch to take
**both-chamber** measures, where one measure yields an Assembly roll *and* a Senate roll.

## Why these four (one later retracted)

The worklist ranks by Assembly-roll value (all 80 Assembly seats are on the November ballot
against 20 of 40 Senate seats). These are the first four at the top whose subject is nameable and
whose stance is defensible under a real research area.

| measure | status | rolls | subject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ~~AB 1078~~ | chaptered | S 29-10, A 57-20 | **retracted on review** — runs both ways; batch-01 had already dropped it |
| AB 1116 | enrolled | S 29-9, A 59-19 | online voter registration without a driver's license |
| AB 1249 | chaptered | S 30-10, A 60-18 | early voting and satellite locations |
| AB 1324 | vetoed | S 30-10, A 60-15 | CalWORKs eligibility |

All three statuses in the final-text rule appear here, so all three closing forms are exercised.

## Yield

**234 live records from 6 rolls** after the AB 1078 retraction (312 from 8 were imported; 78 were
retired). **68 per Assembly roll against 11 per Senate roll** — the 6:1 ratio the worklist
ordering assumes, now measured rather than estimated.
