# batch-20 — what was decided and why

## Bills kept with their weakness stated, not hidden

- **AB 1792** requires the curriculum commission only to *consider* adding dating abuse and online
  abuse content. No school has to teach anything. The description says so in its last sentence.
  A duty to consider is still a duty, which is why it was kept where SB 57 and AB 1439 were not.
- **AB 1838** requires bidders to disclose wage and hour violations, but disqualification is
  discretionary, only final determinations count, and matters under appeal are protected. The
  description ends by noting the bill never says a disclosed violation is itself grounds to reject
  a bid.
- **AB 1655** would keep a family's cash aid intact during immigration detention, but not before
  July 2027, not until the state welfare computer system can handle it, and without the automatic
  county funding that normally follows. All three limits are in the description.

## AB 1629 cuts both ways on one axis and not on another

The bill makes dental plans pay out-of-network dentists directly, and in the same act creates
three new grounds to discipline those same dentists for failing to disclose costs or overcharging
before the plan pays. Read as dentists against insurers, it genuinely cuts both ways.

Read as consumer protection, which is the axis the label uses, it does not: every provision either
gets the patient's own dentist paid, tells the patient the cost first, or caps what the patient
can be charged up front. The label is `corporate_accountability` and the description covers the
duties landing on dentists as well as on plans.

## Four measures dropped for having no real effect

AB 1647 declares itself "declaratory of existing law" and then carves out sentencing, which is
where a juvenile's transfer-hearing statements can hurt most. AB 2015 is a pilot study in one
unnamed region with permissive recommendations and a sunset. AB 2161 implements the federal
Medi-Cal work requirement rather than opposing it, and lets the department defer every protection
it adds if no money is appropriated. AB 1818 moves one procedural determination to a neutral board
and fits no research area.

## The lint caught a 57-word sentence and then a 50-word one

AB 1629's six required disclosures ran as a single list. The first split still came in at 50 words
against the 45-word limit, and the driver refused to judge the batch until the second split. That
is the check working: a batch cannot reach the database with a sentence a reader cannot follow.

## Verification

Lint: 22 descriptions, 0 warnings, longest sentence 41 words. Three British spellings were
corrected before judging.
