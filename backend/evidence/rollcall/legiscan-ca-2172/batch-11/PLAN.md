## Reading was delegated, judging was not

Digests were read by research agents working from a fixed template: every numbered digest item,
every "as specified" pointer chased, and a mandatory **DIRECTION** field asking whether the bill
cuts both ways. Area tagging, filter 5 and all drafting stayed in the main session. The direction
field is what the AB 1078 retraction taught us to ask for up front.

## Nine measures read and dropped

Reading twenty measures produced eleven judgments. The nine drops are recorded with reasons in
`../worklist/ledger.json` — four budget or trailer vehicles, four that cut both ways, and one with
no defensible research area.
