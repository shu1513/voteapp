## A rule settled here: substantive single-county bills are kept, procedural ones are dropped

Earlier batches dropped single-county measures without stating the test. This batch makes it
explicit, because two single-county bills were kept and two were dropped in the same pass.

- **Kept:** SB 1414 (San Bernardino redistricting commission) and SB 1379 (Riverside coroner and
  jail-death reporting), alongside AB 1441 (Merced) from batch-13. Each makes a substantive policy
  choice a voter can hold a legislator to.
- **Dropped:** AB 2484 (San Diego transit tax ballot access) and SB 1408 (Contra Costa tax above
  the state cap), alongside AB 1919 and AB 1923 earlier. Each only decides how or when a local
  measure reaches the ballot.

Every description for a kept single-county bill ends by naming the county, so no reader mistakes
it for statewide law.
