# batch-21 — what was decided and why

## Contingent duties: kept when someone is bound, dropped when nobody is

Two measures in this pass are gated on a future appropriation, and they went opposite ways.

**AB 2599 was kept.** Once funded, a covered company must search its records and file a sworn
affidavit, and must certify that filing to bid on a state contract. A real party carries a real
duty. The description ends by saying none of it applies unless the Legislature appropriates money.

**AB 450 was dropped.** It creates a panel, a study and two reports, all conditional on money the
bill does not provide, and repeals itself in 2029. It ends in recommendations, not services. No
company, agency or person is bound to do anything for anyone.

The same reasoning kept SB 1414 in batch-15 and dropped SB 57, AB 1439 and AB 2015 earlier.

## AB 321 keeps its label despite a real brake

The bill lets a judge reduce a wobbler to a misdemeanor at any point before trial rather than only
at the preliminary hearing. In the same section it provides that once such a motion is denied, a
further one requires a showing of changed circumstances. That brake is new and it restricts the
very relief the bill expands.

It was kept because the brake governs repeat attempts rather than the availability of the relief,
and because the bill reclassifies nothing on its own — it only changes when a discretion the judge
already holds may be used. Both points are in the description, so a reader can weigh the trade.

## Four measures dropped for having no research area that fits

AB 2634, AB 339 and AB 393 are all labor bills: a union-linked scoring preference in grants, notice
to unions before contracting out, and a civil service preference for prison clinicians. Each pushes
consistently one way, and each is well drafted. The research area list simply has no entry for
labor or union rights, and forcing one of the existing areas onto them would tell a reader
something the vote does not support.

This is worth recording plainly, because it is a gap in the area list rather than a defect in the
bills. Nine measures across the campaign have now been dropped for this reason alone.
