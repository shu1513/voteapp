## A line drawn here: a mandatory duty is judgeable, a permissive one is not

Three bills in this pass do little more than require a report. Two were kept and one was dropped,
and the difference is whether anyone is actually obliged to act.

- **Kept:** SB 541 requires the Energy Commission to analyze and publish; AB 93 in batch-13
  required data centers to disclose water use. A duty exists, even if no target follows.
- **Dropped:** SB 57 says the utilities commission *may* assess data center cost shifting. It can
  decline, and the section expires in 2031. Nothing is owed to anyone, so there is no stance to
  record.

SB 177, read in the same pass, sits on the keep side of that line and is held for the next batch.
