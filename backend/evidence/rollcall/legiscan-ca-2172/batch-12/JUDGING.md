# batch-12 — what was decided and why

## Titles that hide the subject

Three bills in this batch would mislead a reader who saw only the title, and the descriptions are
written to fix that:

- **AB 1806** reads as a general police-shooting bill. It extends the existing duty to immigration
  enforcement officers, and it says on its face that it does not regulate federal agents. Both
  facts are in the description, because the second one limits how much the first can reach.
- **AB 1854** reads as a criminal-law bill. It is a shield law for abortion and gender-affirming
  care, and its newest part reaches banks and other financial companies.
- **AB 2128** reads as an administrative housing bill. It bans time limits and work requirements
  on public housing tenants.

## Bills kept despite large carve-outs

**AB 1807** bars immigration enforcement from staging on state land, then exempts existing leases,
corrections operations, and Caltrans land used for transit or sitting at a federal port of entry,
and preserves lawful judicial warrants. **AB 2128** exempts federal Moving to Work agencies and
leaves the federal community service requirement standing.

In both cases the exceptions mark where the bill declines to act. They do not push the other way,
so the label stands and the description lists them.

## Two labels on one measure

AB 1854 carries both `womens_reproductive_rights` and `civil_rights`, because the shield covers
abortion and gender-affirming care together and neither area alone describes the vote. AB 1049 in
batch-11 was double-labelled for the same reason.
