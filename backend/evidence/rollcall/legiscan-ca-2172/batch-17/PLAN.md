# California batch-17 — three measures on corporate accountability

A small batch. Twenty measures were read to produce it; seventeen were dropped, the highest drop
rate of the campaign so far, because this stretch of the worklist is dense with budget trailer
bills and local tax-ballot measures.

| measure | status | subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SB 36 | vetoed | price gouging after a declared emergency |
| SB 177 | chaptered | options for billing large employers whose workers use Medi-Cal |
| SB 327 | enrolled | utilities charging ratepayers to fight public takeover |

All three carry `corporate_accountability`, which fits the area description: holding companies to
legal compliance, consumer protection and public impact.

## Why so many drops here

Six were budget trailer bills from the Committee on Budget and Fiscal Review — SB 168, SB 170,
SB 171, SB 158, SB 164, SB 127. Four were local tax-ballot measures. Five cut both ways. Reasons
are recorded per measure in the ledger.
