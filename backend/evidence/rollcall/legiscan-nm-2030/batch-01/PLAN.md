## Dropped

- **HB 1**, the feed bill, which pays for the legislature's own session.
- **HB 2**, the general appropriation act.
- **HB 505**, the capital outlay bill.
- **SB 16**, executive reorganization, renaming the human services department. Administrative plumbing.
- **SB 351**, which lets a legislative council member sit as a voting member on an interim committee. Internal housekeeping.
- **SB 442**, which raises the pay of elected state officers. Reads either as paying officials properly or as spending on themselves, with no honest single direction.
- **HB 228**, the Improvement Special Assessment Act, which replaces a solar-specific county assessment with a general one. The repeal and the replacement point opposite ways.
