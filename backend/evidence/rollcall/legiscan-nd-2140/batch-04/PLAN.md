# North Dakota batch-04 — plan

9 measures, 9 roll calls, 227 records. **The failed-vote scope**: measures a chamber voted
down. The vote is the record; none of these became law.

## Why a failed vote is worth recording

A recorded position on a bill that lost is often the most decision-relevant thing a
legislator did, and the enacted gate hides it entirely. Pennsylvania established the scope
and imported three one-vote defeats on that reasoning. In a supermajority state the effect
is larger still: this batch supplies six research areas the North Dakota enacted pool never
produced, among them `womens_reproductive_rights`, `gun_control` and `immigration`.

## Version care

Each description was written from the print that chamber voted, selected by date against
the bill's text stack. This matters here: **HB 1145 was amended before the vote**, moving
the required display from every classroom to a prominent place in each school cafeteria.
The description says cafeteria, because that is what the House voted on.

## Dropped from this scope after screening

- **HB 1605**, prohibiting water fluoridation, and **HB 1457**, **HB 1467** and **HB 1519**,
  all vaccine-adjacent. These follow the operator's HB 1454 decision and the standing rule
  that the mainstream-consensus tiebreak is not available on these subjects.
- **HB 1286** and **HB 1377**, both campaign finance recodifications that repeal and replace
  the existing chapter. Direction cannot be read off a recodification without a full
  comparison, which this pass did not do, so they are recorded as not read rather than
  judged on a skim.
