## HB 2013
Read from the enrolled committee substitute (`ls_text.py`). New § 5F-2-9: from
July 1, 2025, new hires in the Bureau of Senior Services and the Departments of
Administration, Environmental Protection, Revenue and Veterans' Assistance are
exempt from the classified civil service system and the state grievance
procedure. Current workers keep both only while they stay in their current
position; one who moves to another job in these agencies becomes exempt. The
finding cites "flexibility" in promotion, layoff, removal, discipline and pay.
Taking away grievance and civil service protection narrows worker protections,
so a yes vote is `against`.

Review correction (2026-09-11): the first descriptions read as if every new hire
lost both protections. § 5F-2-9(c) lets the commissioner or secretary keep an
employee's civil service or grievance status where needed to comply with
federal law, federal regulation or federal funding conditions, so the
descriptions now carry that one clause. § 5F-2-9(d), which keeps governor-
appointed positions out of subsection (b), is not mentioned: those positions
were never in the classified service, so nothing changes for them and the
clause would only add noise for voters. The direction stands: the general rule
strips both protections from new hires, and (c) is a discretionary compliance
carve-out, not a right.
