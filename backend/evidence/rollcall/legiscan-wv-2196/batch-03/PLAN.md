# West Virginia 2025 regular session, batch 03

## What this batch is

One measure: SB 587, "Relating generally to government contracting".

Batch 01 dropped this measure as **deferred rather than guessed at**. It is a
40,000 character rewrite of the state's competitive bidding law, and naming one
honest direction needed a section-by-section read that batch 01 did not do. That
read has now been done, so the measure is judged here rather than left open.

This is the last unjudged closely divided roll call in West Virginia. With it,
every measure-chamber slot in the state that has a closely divided final vote —
115 of them across both sessions — carries a written disposition.

## Which roll call, and which text

The Senate voted three times on this bill. Only the last one is closely divided
and only the last one is that chamber's vote on the text that became law:

- Senate roll 183, 26 March 2025, 34-0. Not divided.
- House roll 513, 11 April 2025, 92-1. Not divided.
- **Senate roll 553, 12 April 2025, 27-7.** The Senate agreeing to the House
  version. This is the record.

The Senate was agreeing to the second committee substitute as amended on the
House floor, which is the enrolled text — the clean final law. The House made
three changes that day which the Senate accepted: the entry price for the new
contracting method rose from $15 million to $20 million, bids became binding for
90 days instead of 60, and public notices became formal newspaper legal
advertisements.

## Date check

The roll call falls on 12 April 2025, the session's final day, so its date was
checked rather than trusted. West Virginia's own vote sheet for roll 553 is
headed "Saturday, April 12, 2025" and prints "Yea: 27  Nay: 7", which matches
the dataset exactly. No `official_vote_date` override is needed.
