# Oregon batch-13: labor measures reopened (labor phase C)

LegiScan session 2191. **6 roll calls, 3 measures, 167 records, 60 candidates.**

labor or union rights. The `labor_rights` area now exists (migration 277, PR
#1294), so each was reread in its enrolled text and judged under the scope rules
in `labor-rights-backfill-2026-09-10/NOTES.md`. All three became law.

## Finality

- HB 2688: the House passed the B-engrossed text 31-22 and the Senate passed it
  unamended 16-13.
- HB 2944: the Senate amended it (B-engrossed) and passed it 16-11, and the
  House concurred 35-21. The House's first vote, 33-24, was on the earlier text.
- HB 3194: the House passed the A-engrossed text 37-16 and the Senate passed it
  unamended 17-10.

HB 3789 stays dropped: it moves protections both ways.
