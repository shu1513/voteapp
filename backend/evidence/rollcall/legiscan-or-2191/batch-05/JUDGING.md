## ⚠ A third superseded staff summary, and this one changed a number

**HB 3865's staff summary says the quiet hours are "9 a.m. to 7 p.m."** The
enrolled Act says **8 a.m. to 8 p.m.**, and adds an exception the summary does
not mention: the hour limit and the three-calls-a-day limit both lift where
the seller has an **established business relationship**, defined as a
transaction in the preceding 18 months. Writing from the summary would have
put a wrong time window and a missing exception into 42 records.

That is now three measures in five batches (SB 243, HB 2385, HB 3865) where
the summary on file describes a version that did not become law. **In Oregon
the staff measure summary is an index, never the source.**

## Scope limits carried into the descriptions

- **HB 3144** is **forward-looking only**. It voids covenants and homeowner
  association rules blocking manufactured or prefabricated homes, but only in
  documents signed, adopted or amended on or after the effective date. It does
  not clear restrictions already recorded, and the description says so.
- **HB 3932** does not protect beavers generally. The ban on taking them for
  sport or sale bites only where a waterway is listed as impaired under the
  federal Clean Water Act *and* it runs through or beside public land, or
  public land lies within 200 feet of a river or stream. Land management staff
  may still remove a beaver threatening roads, culverts or crops, with
  non-lethal methods preferred. **None of it reaches private land.**
- **HB 2964** loans may not be used to buy land, and only nonprofits, tribes
  and similar bodies may borrow.
- **SB 598** lets plans keep their usual controls on the non-opioid
  alternative — prior approval, step therapy, copays, deductibles — so long as
  they are not tighter than the controls on the matching opioid. Saying only
  that plans "must cover" an alternative would overstate it.
