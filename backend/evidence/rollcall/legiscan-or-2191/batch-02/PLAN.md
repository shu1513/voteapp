## Roll selection and version check

All eight rolls are on the text that became law.

- **HB 2138.** The House passed the B-Engrossed bill 33-17, the Senate amended
  it to C-Engrossed and passed that 21-8, and the House concurred 31-12. The
  chosen House roll is the **concurrence**, not the earlier passage, because
  only the concurrence is on the enacted text.
- **SB 551.** Same shape in reverse: Senate passed A-Engrossed, the House
  amended to B-Engrossed and passed it 33-19, the Senate concurred 22-8. The
  chosen Senate roll is the concurrence.
- **HB 3054** and **HB 2586** were each passed in identical form by both
  chambers ("Do pass the A-Eng. bill"), so their single rolls stand.

The superseded check was run over all eight rolls before any description was
written. Two measures had an earlier divided roll superseded by a later
concurrence; taking the later roll in each case meant no
`acknowledge_later_rolls` was needed anywhere.
