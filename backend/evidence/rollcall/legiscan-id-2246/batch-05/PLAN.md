## Version check

Senate Bill 1352 and Senate Bill 1354 were amended in the Senate before it
voted, so both chambers' kept rolls sit on the engrossed text. `S1352E1` and
`S1354E1` were read, not the introduced prints. The other six have no engrossed
print, so the introduced text became law.

## Truncated tallies confirmed

Four kept rolls sit on Idaho's `Rules Suspended:` lines, which lose their tally
in the feed. All four were read from the bill pages first: House Bill 508 house
39-29-2, House Bill 776 house 49-18-3, Senate Bill 1352 house 36-34-0, and
Senate Bill 1354 house 47-23-0.
