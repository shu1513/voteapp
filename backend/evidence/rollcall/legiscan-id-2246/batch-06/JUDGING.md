## House Bill 562 — chapter 201, insurance notice

Raises the notice an insurer must give before cancelling a policy from thirty
days to sixty, and before declining to renew from forty-five days to sixty. A
late nonrenewal notice now extends cover for sixty days rather than forty-five.
Corporate accountability, a yes vote is for, because the act shifts time from
the insurer to the policyholder.

## House Bill 810 — chapter 307, legislator residency

Adds a requirement that a candidate for the legislature have kept a fixed home
inside the district boundaries for the year before the general election, on top
of the existing requirement to be a registered voter there. Election integrity,
a yes vote is for.

## House Bill 849 — chapter 173, career technical teacher pay

Credits industry experience when placing a career technical teacher with an
occupational specialist certificate on the pay ladder, from one year's credit
for two or three years of industry work up to four years' credit for eight or
more. Existing career technical staff on the lowest rung must be moved up where
the rule puts them higher, and the act sets out how they advance afterwards.
Public education quality, a yes vote is for.

## Senate Bill 1257 — chapter 131, child protection visits

Defines visitation to cover video, telephone and written contact as well as
meeting in person, and bars in-person visits where the department has
substantiated a claim of sexual or physical abuse against the parent, guardian
or custodian, unless a court finds them in the child's best interest and writes
conditions keeping them safe. Also revises the grounds for ending parental
rights. Social programs and welfare, a yes vote is for.

## Senate Bill 1269 — chapter 86, cloud seeding

Creates authorization and reporting duties for cloud seeding projects and limits
state money to basins where the water board finds supplies short. It also
exempts approved seeding from state and local permits and from trespass and
nuisance claims short of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

The direction was argued. The immunity and permit exemption cut against the
label, but the act's operative core is a state water augmentation programme with
a finding requirement and yearly reports. Environment and public health, a yes
vote is for. The description states the immunity plainly so a reader can weigh
it.

## Senate Bill 1294 — chapter 266, newborn hearing screening

Requires every newborn to be screened for hearing loss before discharge, or
within twenty-one days for a birth outside a facility, using an approved
physiologic method, with failed screenings reported within seven days so
follow-up can start. The duty lasts only while federal funding continues, which
the description says. Healthcare affordability, a yes vote is for.

## Senate Bill 1300 — chapter 267, agency directors

Moves the appointment of the directors of Fish and Game, Transportation, and
Parks and Recreation from their commissions and boards to the governor, with
each serving at the governor's pleasure.

The direction was argued. Critics say it politicizes wildlife and highway
management; sponsors say it makes directors answerable to an elected official.
Government efficiency, a yes vote is for, which is the sponsors' rationale and
is what the act does on its face. The description says the commissions no longer
pick or hold the director, which is the part critics object to.

## Senate Bill 1311 — chapter 134, ignition interlocks

Requires the interlock vendor to notify the prosecuting authority every time a
driver is blocked from starting the car, and to prove installation to the
Transportation Department. Writes the camera into the device definition and
leaves the blocking level at .025. Public safety and crime control, a yes vote
is for.
