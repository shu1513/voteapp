## Finality

The Senate amended H 516 and passed it 20-14. The House concurred and passed the
Senate version 43-24. Both rolls are on the text that became law (Session Law
Chapter 342). The House's first vote, 59-10, was on the earlier text and is not
closely divided.

## Dropped

- H 645, Portable Benefit Plan Act (Senate 25-7): it lets hiring parties pay
  into benefit accounts for independent contractors, but also says those
  payments are not evidence that the worker is an employee. It moves protections
  both ways, the same call as Wisconsin AB 269, so it gets no label.
