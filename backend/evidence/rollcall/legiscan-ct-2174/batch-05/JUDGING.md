## HB 6844
The House passed it as amended by House Amendment A (LCO 9133), which struck
the file copy and substituted new text. Under that text, section 7-450c bars a
town or special taxing district from cutting a retiree's pension because of
permanent partial disability benefits received from July 1, 2025. It also
counts temporary total and temporary partial disability benefits as wages in the
pension calculation, capped at the worker's pay before the injury, and excludes
the state-run municipal employees' plan. Section 2 orders a Comptroller study of
moving police and firefighters into a defined pension plan.

## SB 1222
The Senate passed the file copy unamended (Amendment A was rejected). It adds
public works department employees to the "portal to portal" coverage in section
31-275: travel between home and work is covered when they are on emergency call,
ordered in while nonessential staff are excused, or after two or more days in a
row of mandatory overtime.

## SB 1371
The Senate passed the file copy unamended (Amendments A and B were rejected).
Section 10-151 would require just cause to end a teacher's contract. Dismissal
hearings for tenured teachers would go only to a neutral hearing officer, whose
decision binds both sides, with costs shared. The rule letting a board reverse
a nonrenewal only when it was arbitrary and capricious would be struck.
