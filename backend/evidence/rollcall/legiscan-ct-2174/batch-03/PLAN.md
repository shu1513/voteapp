## Why this batch is House-only

Connecticut's Senate desc names no question — every Senate roll reads
`Senate Roll Call Vote <n>` — and `roll_call_id` order is not time order inside
a same-day Senate batch. Of the 48 divided Senate rolls on measures that did not
become law, the tallies show most are rejected minority floor amendments
(11-25, 12-24, 10-24 and so on), and the feed gives no way to map a tally to a
named action when a bill has several the same day. The House rolls carry the
chamber's own sequential vote number, which is chronological, so they can be
aligned to the action trail. Senate rolls in this pool are left open with that
reason recorded, not silently skipped.

## Dropped from this pool

- **HB 5002** (housing and the needs of homeless persons, vetoed, 84-67) and
  **HB 8002** (housing growth, 90-56). Both are the marquee housing votes of the
  session and both are dropped for the same reason: the enacted text runs to
  185,000 and 250,000 characters across many subjects, and filter 3 asks for one
  thing a voter can recognize. They deserve a dedicated read rather than a
  paragraph written from section one.
- **HB 5607** (task force to study eliminating the subminimum wage for people
  with disabilities), **HB 6841**, **HB 6908**, **HB 6999** — study and task
  force bills with no operative effect.
- **HB 7158** (safety plans for minor patients), **HB 6040** (state historical
  commission), **HB 6844** (municipal pension systems), **HB 8004** (children's
  behavioral health plus an unrelated self-employment expense standard) — no
  single defensible direction.
