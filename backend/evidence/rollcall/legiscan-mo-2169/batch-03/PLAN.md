# MO batch-03 — the divided votes that did not become law

**5 divided House roll calls / 5 measures / 521 records.**

Batches 01 and 02 finished Missouri's divided-and-enacted pool. This batch opens
the pool the campaign had not touched here: divided floor votes on measures that
did not become law, following the Pennsylvania batch-02 precedent. All five
passed the House and died in the Senate.

| measure | area | yea | House |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HB 742 bars state spending on diversity, equity and inclusion | civil_rights | against | 108-50 |
| HB 544 federal pesticide label satisfies any cancer warning duty | corporate_accountability | against | 85-72 |
| HB 68 personal injury lawsuits move from a five-year to a two-year deadline | corporate_accountability | against | 92-42 |
| HB 918 product liability requires proving the exact product | corporate_accountability | against | 94-57 |
| HB 565 wider shield for horse and livestock activities | corporate_accountability | against | 97-51 |

**HB 544 at 85-72 is the closest recorded vote in the Missouri run.** Four of the
five are a single coherent strand: limits on who can be sued and for how long.

## Read and dropped

- **HB 575** initiative petitions and referendums, and **HB 970** gaming
  regulations and video lottery terminals. Both repeal a dozen or more sections
  and enact as many again, and both read two ways — one side calls HB 575
  protection of the constitution from outside money, the other calls it a block
  on direct democracy. No direction the text settles.
- **HB 875** protections for belief-based student associations at public
  colleges — protects association rights and permits exclusion from membership
  at the same time.
- **HB 711** student transfers to nonresident districts — school-choice
  financing, dropped on standing precedent.
- **HB 903**, **HB 780**, **HB 629**, **HB 660** property assessment and taxing
  district mechanics; **HB 572** motor fuel tax rate machinery; **HB 837**,
  **HB 606**, **HB 835**, **HB 939** administrative changes. No nameable single
  subject with a direction.
- Senate rolls in this pool are left open: seven Senate seats remain unlinkable
  in the crosswalk, as `../README.md` records, so a Senate roll reaches few or
  no candidates.
