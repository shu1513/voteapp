## Dropped, after reading the enacted text

- **SB 344** — a vehicle bill, and the reason this batch checks every one.
  LegiScan still titles it "Sales and Use Taxes; firearms, ammunition, gun
  safes ... exempt", and that is what the Senate passed on 6 February 2024. The
  enacted text is something else entirely: excluding broadband investment grants
  from corporate taxable income. Same pattern as SB 33 in the 2025-2026 batch.
- **SB 189** — the 2024 elections omnibus. Dropped under filter 3. Its caption
  runs to sixteen separate subjects, from removing the Secretary of State from
  the State Election Board to third-party ballot access, elector challenges,
  ballot tabulation marks, absentee chain of custody and an optical character
  recognition pilot. There is no single nameable subject and no single
  direction.
- **HB 1172** (removing public trust doctrine references from water rights law),
  **HB 1146** (forcing water permits for private companies where no public
  service is coming), **SB 62** (public camping ordinances and moving homeless
  people between counties), **HB 481** (pension fiduciary duty and proxy
  voting), **SB 74** (protective orders for senior executives alongside bans on
  false legal advertising) — each reads two ways on its own text, so each fails
  filter 5.
- **HB 1409** and **SB 159's Senate roll** — dropped on the version check, see
  JUDGING.md.
