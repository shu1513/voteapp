# Federal labor measures: judging

Each measure read from its passed text on govinfo.gov (House engrossed, or
Senate engrossed for S.J.Res. 29).

## H.R. 842, PRO Act (House 117-1 roll 70)
Adds an employee test to the National Labor Relations Act: a worker is an
employee unless free from control, working outside the usual course of the
business, and in an independent trade. Makes it an unfair labor practice to
threaten or permanently replace strikers. Adds civil penalties of up to $10,000
per violation of Board orders, first-contract mediation and arbitration, and
section 111 permits fair share agreements despite state right-to-work laws.
Widens the right to organize: `for`.

## H.R. 1195 (House 117-1 roll 118)
Directs the Labor Secretary to issue an OSHA standard requiring health care and
social service employers (hospitals, nursing homes, clinics, psychiatric and
community care settings) to write and follow workplace violence prevention
plans with direct care employees and their representatives. Workplace safety:
`for`.

## S.J.Res. 29 (Senate 117-1 roll 489)
Disapproves the Department of Labor's "COVID-19 Vaccination and Testing;
Emergency Temporary Standard" (86 Fed. Reg. 61402), an OSHA workplace safety
standard. Removing a workplace safety rule: `against`. The House never voted.

## H.R. 2499 (House 117-2 roll 149)
New 5 U.S.C. 8143b: for federal employees with five years in fire protection,
listed cancers and chronic lung disease are presumed work-caused for workers'
compensation; so is a sudden cardiac event or stroke, but only while, or within
24 hours after, firefighting or emergency response (section 8143b(a)(2)(N)).
Widens workers' compensation: `for`. The Senate never voted on this bill, but
the same text was enacted as section 5305 of the FY2023 defense authorization
(Pub. L. 117-263, December 23, 2022), so the sentences say the provisions
became law rather than "did not become law".

## H.R. 903 (House 117-2 roll 172)
Moves TSA screeners into the title 5 personnel system, so chapter 71 (labor
relations) and chapter 77 (appeals) apply. Section 6 keeps the federal strike
ban. Widens bargaining rights: `for`.

## H.R. 6087 (House 117-2 roll 233)
Adds nurse practitioners and physician assistants as eligible providers under
the Federal Employees' Compensation Act. Widens workers' compensation access:
`for`. The tally, 325-83, clears the divided-vote gate (the losing side is at
least a quarter of the winning side).

## H.R. 302 (House 117-2 roll 432)
Bars excepting competitive service positions except into Schedules A to E as in
effect on September 30, 2020, which blocks new schedules outside the merit
system. Protects civil service jobs: `for`, matching the West Virginia civil
service judgments in this phase.

## H.R. 1948 (House 117-2 roll 530)
Strikes 38 U.S.C. 7422(b) to (d), which let the VA Secretary exclude clinical
matters, professional conduct and pay setting from bargaining by VA health
professionals. Widens bargaining: `for`.

## H.R. 2550 (House 119-1 roll 332)
Nullifies the March 27, 2025 executive order "Exclusions from Federal
Labor-Management Relations Programs" and bars funds to carry it out. Contracts
in effect on March 26, 2025 stay in force for their terms. Restores bargaining:
`for`.
