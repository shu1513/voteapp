## What was set aside, and why

Eight measures were read in full; three did not survive.

- **HB 480** (jury trial in constitutional challenges) — filter 5. It creates a
  real right, and the House split 50-49 over it, but no research area maps to
  court procedure with a defensible direction.
- **HB 591** (Freedom Week in schools) — filter 5. It names no document to
  teach, sets no hours or grades, and carries no penalty and no money. It also
  **struck** "during the school day" from the existing commemorative-days rule.
- **HB 636** (marijuana edibles) — filter 5. It halves the THC per serving from
  10 mg to 5 mg. A narrow product-potency rule.

## Every roll was checked against Montana's own vote record

All eight imported rolls, and every other roll on all eight measures read, were
compared member by member against
`api.legmt.gov/bills/v1/votes/findByBillId`. **All agree exactly.**

One near miss worth recording: HB 492's earlier House third reading, roll
1503251 of 3 March, **is** one of the rolls where LegiScan puts a member on the
wrong side. It is superseded by the 22 April vote this batch uses, so it was
never a candidate — but it is a reminder that the check has to run on the roll
actually selected, every time.

## Reach

The House rolls carry 74 to 75 records each and the Senate rolls carry 11 each,
because all 100 House seats are on the 2026 ballot while only about half the
Senate is.
