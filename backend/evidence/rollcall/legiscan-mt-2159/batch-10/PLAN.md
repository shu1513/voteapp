# Montana batch-10 — the administrative tail, and a change of method

Eight measures, thirteen roll calls, 462 candidate records. All eight became law.

**Thirty-three measures were read for this batch and twenty-five were dropped.**
That is a far lower keep rate than batches 7 to 9, and it is the point of this
batch: the remaining bills are mostly agency housekeeping, and the way to work
them is in grouped triages rather than one full read each.

**HB 15 carries its Senate roll only.** Its House third reading is one of the
seven rolls held on the LegiScan vote defect — LegiScan has Randyn Gregg voting
yes where Montana has him voting no. The Senate roll checks clean.

HB 338 and HB 908 carry one chamber because the other chamber's last kept floor
vote was lopsided.

## The method change

Working the queue in bill-number order was wasteful: the low numbers are agency
housekeeping, and the first eleven bills read produced one keep. So all 249
remaining bills were classified by subject. **137 are administrative** and 112
carry real subject matter. From here the campaign works in two phases —
substantive bills in themed batches, administrative bills in grouped triages of
about ten, with only the ones that turn out to matter promoted to a full read.

This batch proved the triage out. Three grouped triages covered 22 bills between
them and promoted seven to a proper judgment, at a fraction of the cost of 22
individual reads.

## What was set aside, and why

Twenty-five drops, all under filter 5. Full reasoning in
`../survey/filter-5-drops.md`. In short:

- **Six appropriations bills** — HB 2, HB 3, HB 4 fund "various state agencies"
  with no single subject; HB 9, HB 10 and HB 12 are purely money.
- **Nine pension and agency bills** — HB 46, HB 61, HB 62, HB 67, HB 68, HB 72,
  HB 81, HB 85, plus HB 13 the state pay plan.
- **Four education bills** — HB 24, HB 266, HB 595, HB 602, HB 866.
- **Five building, land and tax bills** — HB 144, HB 614, HB 714, HB 846, SB 33.

## Two coordination instructions, one of which fired

- **HB 846 section 8**: if SB 260 also passed, SB 260 sections 10 to 14 are
  void. **SB 260 was signed on 12 May 2025, so this fired.** Recorded in the
  campaign checkpoint, because SB 260 must not later be judged as though those
  sections were law.
- **HB 266 section 2**: if SB 258 also passed, SB 258 is void. SB 258 was
  **vetoed**, so this did not fire.

## Every roll was checked against Montana's own vote record

All thirteen imported rolls, and every other roll on all thirty-three measures
read, were compared member by member against
`api.legmt.gov/bills/v1/votes/findByBillId`. **All agree exactly.**

## Reach

The House rolls carry 74 to 75 records each and the Senate rolls carry 11 each,
because all 100 House seats are on the 2026 ballot while only about half the
Senate is.
