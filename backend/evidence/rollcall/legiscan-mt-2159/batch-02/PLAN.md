# Montana batch-02 — what was selected and why

Seven measures, fourteen roll calls, all from the 2025 Regular Session. All
seven became law. Two more were read in full and dropped.

## How these were chosen

The same five filters as batch-01, applied to what batch-01 left: 613 divided
roll calls on 364 measures that became law. Within that pool the batch was
picked to be **area-coherent** — environment, then health care — so the reading
stayed on related statutes, and to open research areas Montana had no coverage
in.

Joint resolutions stay excluded by rule. Montana joint resolutions never go to
the governor and are not law, so no description of one could say the measure
became law. 38 of the remaining measures are joint resolutions.

`corporate_accountability` is new to Montana. The three environment measures all
point the same way, which is the majority agenda in a Republican trifecta —
the same shape Maine and Connecticut showed from the other direction.

## Which roll each chamber gets

Filter 4 takes the chamber's decisive vote. Three measures needed care:

- **HB 703 and HB 740** were amended by the Senate and came back. The House's
  decisive vote is the later `3rd Reading Passed as Amended by Senate`, not its
  own earlier passage. For HB 740 that distinction matters a lot: the House
  passed its own version **98-1**, then split **63-35** on the Senate's version.
  Taking the first roll would have recorded near-unanimity on a measure that
  actually divided the chamber. Its description names both tallies.
- **SB 319** went to a conference committee, so both selected rolls are the
  April 30 votes adopting the conference report.

## Dropped under filter 5 after a full read

- **HB 218**, optometrist laser and in-office surgery. Access improves, but the
  objection is patient safety, and quality of care is inside the health care
  area's own definition.
- **SB 497**, provider career fatigue and wellness. The wellness program is
  paired with civil immunity and an evidentiary privilege over impairment
  records — a counter-strand with its own sections.

Both are recorded in `../survey/filter-5-drops.md`.

## Held for their own batch

The four big environment bills — **HB 285** and **SB 221** (both rewriting the
Montana Environmental Policy Act), **HB 664** (numeric nutrient standards) and
**HB 685** (nondegradation) — run to 229 KB of enrolled text between them and
are the session's substantive environmental fight. They get a dedicated batch
rather than a corner of this one. **HB 231**, the property tax rewrite, is
still held for the same reason.
