## What was set aside, and why

Ten measures were read in full; four did not survive.

- **HB 554** (gray wolf reclassification) — filter 5. The clause it deleted had
  never been used. Explained at length in `JUDGING.md`, because the research
  behind it is reusable.
- **HB 623** and **HB 696** (nuclear) — filter 5. Both authorize a nuclear
  facility class while *adding* state environmental review. The subject points
  one way and the mechanism the other.
- **HB 28** (public charter schools) — filter 5. It exempts charter schools from
  the general school-opening rules but also withholds first-year per-student
  money from board-run charters and adds a clawback. Two-sided.

## Every roll was checked against Montana's own vote record

Batch-07 found that LegiScan's Montana feed can put a member on the wrong side
of a vote. All eight rolls imported here, and every other roll on all ten
measures read, were compared member by member against
`api.legmt.gov/bills/v1/votes/findByBillId`. **All agree exactly.**

## Reach

The House rolls carry 74 to 75 records each and the Senate rolls carry 11 each,
because all 100 House seats are on the 2026 ballot while only about half the
Senate is.
