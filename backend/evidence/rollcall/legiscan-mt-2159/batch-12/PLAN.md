## The bill worth reading first

**HB 245 is the most consequential measure in the batch and its title hides
that.** "Revise the Montana HELP Act workforce development provisions and
termination date" reads like housekeeping. Section 2 repeals six sets of session
law sections, and those are the end dates that would have closed the HELP Act —
the programme that provides health coverage to lower-income adults in Montana.
Repealing them leaves the Act with no expiry.

The same act cuts the requirement that the state contract with outside providers
for training in high-demand jobs, replacing it with state-run work assessments.
The record says both things, because a reader deserves both.

## Where a description had to be careful

**HB 825** adds a way for a hospital patient to name someone they trust to decide
for them. It would be easy to describe that as the patient's choice taking
priority. It does not: the statutory order puts a court-appointed guardian and a
formal written appointment first, then spouse, adult child, parent and sibling,
and only then the trusted person the patient named. The record says where in that
order the new option sits.

**HB 804** moves in two directions and the record says so. The threshold at which
a group must report its lobbying rises from $2,150 to $3,000, which narrows who
must report. But reports must now be filed electronically, and the monthly
reporting trigger falls from $5,000 to $3,000, which widens what gets reported.
It is judged "for" `anti_corruption` because two of the three changes increase
disclosure and the threshold move tracks the inflation adjustment already in the
statute.

## Every roll was checked against Montana's own vote record

All seventeen imported rolls, and every other floor roll on the fifteen measures
read, were compared member by member against
`api.legmt.gov/bills/v1/votes/findByBillId`. **All agree exactly.**

## Reach

The House rolls carry about 73 records each and the Senate rolls about 10 or 11,
because all 100 House seats are on the 2026 ballot while only about half the
Senate is.
