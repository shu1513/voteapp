# Alabama 2026 batch-02 — the one-chamber scope

The 2026 half of the one-chamber batch. The full selection reasoning, the gate verification and the
list of everything dropped are in `../../legiscan-al-2148/batch-02/PLAN.md`, because the pool was
worked across both sessions at once.

One measure qualified here: **HB 360**, the Second Amendment Sales Tax Holiday, which the House
passed 72-29 on 2026-02-24 and the Senate never voted on. It scores `gun_control` with yes =
against: the area is about regulating firearm access to reduce gun violence, and an annual sales-tax
exemption on guns, ammunition and hunting supplies (silencers included) cuts the other way.

HB 360 was passed **without amendment** — the history records a previous-question motion and then
passage, with no adopted amendment and no engrossed print — so the introduced text is the text the
House voted.
