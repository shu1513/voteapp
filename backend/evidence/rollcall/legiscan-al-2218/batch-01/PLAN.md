# Alabama 2026 batch-01 — selection plan

## How the batch was selected

The same five filters as the 2025 batch, with one Alabama-2026 step added at the front.

0. **The roll must be filed under the right bill.** This session attaches some physical votes to
   more than one bill (`../CODE-FINDINGS.md` §1), so every candidate roll's printed roll call number
   was checked against its own bill's history. That check removed HB 593's roll 1674185, which
   carries HB 631's number.
1. **Divided** — the losing side is at least a quarter the winning side. 27 of 1,081 kept floor
   votes.
2. **Consequential** — the measure became law. 18 rolls on 14 measures.
3. **A nameable subject** — HB 631 (Etowah County) drops out as a local act.
4. **One roll per measure per chamber, preferring the vote on the enacted text.** SB 57's House vote
   on the conference report replaces its earlier House passage vote.
5. **A defensible direction, or a deliberate no-stance import.**

## When a measure is imported with no stance rather than dropped

Three measures here carry `general` with no stance. The rule applied, and worth reusing: import
without a stance when the vote is divided, enacted and of clear public salience but no research area
carries an honest direction; drop when the measure is both outside the taxonomy and low salience.
HB 2 and SB 5 are high-salience votes that map to no area, and HB 580 is a contested-direction
measure inside the one area that could plausibly hold it. SB 361 (dog tethering) is outside the
taxonomy and drew near-consensus in the Senate, so it was dropped instead.

## Dropped under filter 5, after reading each enacted Act

- **SB 254 (parole revocation).** It grants the parole board discretion for most violations but also
  keeps mandatory revocation for a listed class of serious offences, so the text runs both ways
  inside public safety. Same shape as HB 43 in the 2025 batch.
- **HB 568 (workforce fees).** Child labour certificate fees, boiler and pressure vessel rules, and
  elevator standards in one bill: no single nameable subject.
- **HB 593 (small craft alcohol).** New licence categories for small distilleries and wineries. No
  research area fits an alcohol licensing expansion.
- **SB 361 (dog tethering).** Tethering and shelter standards with misdemeanour penalties. No
  research area covers animal welfare.
- **HB 631 (Etowah County).** Local act, dropped at filter 3.

## Remaining

8 divided rolls sit on measures that passed one chamber and died. Those belong to the one-chamber
scope, which is tracked with the 2025 session's equivalent pool.
