Four areas carry both directions across the two enacted batches, which is what

progressive lines produces.

## How these were chosen

The 34 measures batch-01 left as candidates were triaged from the bill's own
description and the neutral summary in its Committee Statement, and then the
enacted act was read for every measure that survived. All five filters were
applied. Each vote is close, each measure became law on its own history rather
than on LegiScan's status field, each subject is one a voter would recognize,
each is the measure's only Final Reading vote, and each carries an area with a
direction that can be defended.

## Version check, per roll

None of the nine has a text-changing action after its Final Reading vote.

**One trap here is worth naming.** Nebraska posts lines like "Provisions and
portions of LB 125 amended into LB 9 by AM 549", and it posts them *after* the
Governor has signed. A check for "any text-changing action after the roll"
flags all of them. They are notes recording which other bills were folded in by
an amendment adopted earlier, not changes made after the vote.

The notes still matter, because they say what an act really contains. LB 9
absorbed LB 125, which is the same subject, and LB 795 absorbed LB 817, which
is the fentanyl penalty strand already in it. Both are safe. **LB 921 absorbed
four bills and two of them cut the other way, which is why it was dropped.**

## What was dropped, and why

25 of the 34 were dropped, and the reasons are written on each worklist row.
The common ones: several unrelated subjects in one act, no research area that
fits, and a benefit and an objection of comparable weight inside the same area.

Two measures are left as candidates for a later pass rather than dropped on the
merits, because each needs a full read of a long, multi-subject act: **LB 415**,
which narrows the paid sick leave law voters approved in 2024, and **LB 530**,
on motor vehicle homicide and road safety.

With those two, every one of the 49 roll calls in Nebraska's pool now carries a
disposition.
