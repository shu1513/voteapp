## Why this scope exists

**LegiScan marks all three enacted.** They were not. The Governor returned each
one without approval, and the Legislature failed to override two of them and
never voted on the third. A measure's fate has to be read off its own history.

Nebraska is unusual in having no other pool of measures that did not become
law. A bill that loses on General File never reaches a Final Reading vote, so
the one-chamber scope other states opened does not exist here. These vetoes are
the whole of it.

**Nebraska never uses the word veto.** Its history says "Returned by Governor
without approval", and an override is a motion "that the bill becomes law
notwithstanding the objections of the Governor". Searching for the word finds
nothing.

## The two measures dropped from this scope

- **LB 287**, bed bug duties for one city's housing agency. That is habitability
  procedure, which sits outside the housing area on the Maryland HB 767
  precedent.
- **LB 839**, requiring cities to report how many accessible housing units were
  built. A reporting duty and nothing more.

## Wording

Every description is conditional throughout — "would have required", never "the
act". The builder refuses to write a batch-03 description that lacks "would
have" or that calls the bill an act, and that check was proved to fire on a
known-bad string before the file was written.

Each tail states a completed fact rather than a time-stamped hedge, because the
Legislature has adjourned and these bills are dead: two name the failed override
and its tally, and LB 1029's says the Legislature did not vote to override.

## One thing the pipeline cannot reach

**Nebraska takes its override votes as numbered motions**, so LegiScan captions
them `Rountree MO259 failed`, exactly like any other motion, and the config's
rule against motion votes excludes them. Only the bill history names the
question. All four override votes in this Legislature were close — 24-24, 24-24,
19-28 and 22-26 — and none can be imported today. Reaching them would need a
per-roll disposition file, the design already parked for Maine.
